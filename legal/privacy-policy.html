Privacy Policy

LabKitPro Quality Tools (Chrome Extension) · Effective date: 30 October 2026

In short: This extension runs entirely on your device. It does not collect your personal information, does not transmit your measurement data anywhere, and contains no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking. The only data it stores lives locally on your own computer.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the "Cpk, SPC, MSA, ISO Tools" Chrome extension (the "Extension"), independently operated by an individual developer under the name LabKitPro ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, contact us at support@labkitpro.com.

2. Information the Extension processes

The Extension processes only the data you choose to enter or import. We do not ask for, and the Extension does not capture, any personal identifiers such as your name, email address, location, or payment details. There is no account and no login.

  • Analysis inputs: measurement values you type in or import from local files (CSV, TXT, TSV) — for example process-capability data, control-chart readings, gage studies, and attribute judgments.
  • Generated results: the calculations you create and choose to view or export.

3. How your data is stored — and that it never leaves your device

  • All inputs and results are stored locally on your own device using the Chrome Storage API (with a local-storage fallback).
  • The Extension does not send any data to any server. There is no analytics, telemetry, advertising SDK, or remote code of any kind. Every computation takes place on your device.
  • Because nothing is transmitted, we never receive, see, or have access to your data.

4. Links to external websites

The Extension contains links and calls-to-action that open labkitpro.com in a new browser tab only when you click them. That website is governed by its own privacy policy. Following a link does not transmit any of your Extension data.

5. Legal basis for processing (EEA / UK users)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we process the limited information described above on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing a functional offline tool, together with your voluntary submission of data for that purpose. Because no data is transferred outside your device or to any third party, the impact on your privacy is minimal.

6. California residents (CCPA)

We do not sell personal information, and the Extension does not collect personal information as defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act. There is therefore nothing to disclose, access, or delete on our side.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Your data remains on your device until you remove it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the Extension's stored data in your browser, or by uninstalling the Extension — which removes all associated local data.
  • We retain no copy of your data, because we never receive it.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to access, rectify, or erase your data and to object to or restrict its processing. Since your data is stored solely on your own device and is never transmitted to us, you exercise these rights directly through your browser (clear storage or uninstall). We are happy to assist: support@labkitpro.com.

9. Children's privacy

The Extension is a professional tool intended for adult users in quality, manufacturing, and laboratory roles. It is not directed to children.

10. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date above. Material changes will be noted in the Extension where practicable.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: support@labkitpro.com.
