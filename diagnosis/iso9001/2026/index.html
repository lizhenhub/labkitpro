ISO 9001:2026 transition · Reality Diagnosis notes

ISO 9001:2026 was published on 16 September 2026. From 31 March 2028 new initial certificates are issued only to the 2026 edition, and 2015-edition certificates lapse at the 30 September 2029 cut-off. This diagnosis screens your QMS sub-clause by sub-clause against the 2026 text rather than reusing the 2015 checklist, so the gaps it reports are the ones a transition audit actually looks for. The largest differences from a 2015 screen sit in clauses 4 to 7: a recorded climate-change conclusion under 4.1, demonstrable quality culture and ethical behaviour under 5.1, separate risk and opportunity registers under 6.1.2 / 6.1.3, seven-factor change planning under 6.3, and awareness that covers culture rather than procedure recall under 7.3.

What the 2026 screen asks for

Four kinds of evidence are most often missing: a recorded conclusion on climate-change issues in the context analysis (analyze the impact if relevant, or record why it is not — leaving it blank is a gap); a quality culture that is still one sentence in the policy instead of 3-5 observable behaviors with 2-3 measurable indicators such as first-pass yield, rework / cost of quality and awareness surveys; risks and opportunities still merged into one register with no improvement project, owner or due date attached to each opportunity; and a change procedure that omits 6.3 f) monitoring and evaluating effectiveness and g) reviewing the results. The screen also checks whether every internal audit has a defined objective, whether management-review inputs include changes in the needs and expectations of interested parties, and whether awareness training has moved to case-based culture modules with sign-in sheets and quiz records.

Accreditation-side milestones behind the deadline

The official Global ACI-TECH-3-TR 2029-09-30 (M) timeline: accreditation bodies (ABs) are ready and publish their arrangements by 2027-03-31; certification bodies (CBs) submit transition declarations by 2027-06-30; AB transition decisions on CBs are due 2027-09-30, after which a CB may issue 2026-edition certificates; from 2028-03-31 new initial certificates are issued only to the 2026 edition; 2029-09-30 is the cut-off. A transition audit may be combined with a surveillance or recertification audit and may use ICT remote techniques, with any additional audit time justified under ISO/IEC 17021-1 and the corresponding technical documents. Practically: before booking a transition audit, confirm your CB has passed its AB transition decision, otherwise the certificate you receive is not a 2026-edition one.

Using the output

The output is organized as a clause heatmap, evidence gaps, an overall judgment, prioritized actions and a clause-to-document-to-record matrix, and can be exported as the working basis for your gap analysis and corrective-action tracking. Tackle high-severity gaps in clauses 5 and 6 first: those requirements cannot be closed with a single training session — they need rewritten documents, live registers and operating records. By contrast, 8.3 design outputs, 8.4 direct delivery by external providers and 8.5 availability and use of documented information read close to the 2015 text, so treat them as review-and-confirm points rather than rebuilding them. Frequently asked questions

How is a 2026 gap diagnosis different from the 2015 one?

The sub-clause library is rebuilt for the new edition: climate change in 4.1, quality culture and ethical behaviour in 5.1, separate risk (6.1.2) and opportunity (6.1.3) actions, seven-factor change planning in 6.3, upgraded awareness in 7.3, plus a defined objective for every internal audit and a new management-review input on changes in interested parties. Reusing a 2015 checklist misses these new evidence requirements.

Do we need a new certification to transition?

No. Transition happens inside your existing certification cycle, usually as a transition audit combined with the next surveillance or recertification audit — you do not start from scratch. The diagnosis exists to expose gaps early, before you have to produce evidence on audit day.

When should we run this screen?

Working back from 2029-09-30, a first gap analysis and document rewrite around T-24 (2027-09) is sensible; internal audit and management review should be complete by T-12 (2028-09); by T-6 (2029-03) you should be booking the transition audit. Starting early avoids the 2028-2029 peak and tight CB schedules.

Can the exported result be used as audit evidence?

It is internal preparation material for gap analysis and action tracking, not an audit report and not a certification decision. The real evidence is what you produce from it: the context conclusion, the two registers, change-review records, the audit plan and the management-review inputs. This is a preparatory self-check for your own transition work. It produces review comments and recommendations, not an audit report, not a certification or accreditation decision, and it does not replace the transition audit performed by your certification body.
