Reality Diagnosis

Against IATF 16949:2016 Annex-SL clauses 4–10: paste or upload your company material (manual / procedures / audit records…) — we screen audit-point coverage clause by clause and produce a gap heat-map, overall conformity and missing-point lists, exportable to Word.
