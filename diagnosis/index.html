§4–§8 gap heat-map

United States — A2LA · NVLAP. Set your maturity (0 = not started, 5 = fully implemented) for each clause.

§4 公正性 / 保密
Low · w 0.483
Maturity:4 linked docs · 10 FR-25 items
Body focus: A2LA R101 requires a documented conflict-of-interest policy; P106/P120 govern branch & satellite sites. NVLAP requires named Authorized Representative & Approved Signatories and controls use of the 'NVLAP' name/logo (15 CFR 285).
Common nonconformance: Impartiality risk not documented for in-house/commercial labs; signatory authority ambiguous.
§5 组织要求
Low · w 0.441
Maturity:2 linked docs · 10 FR-25 items
Body focus: A2LA P106 (branch systems) / P107 (transfer). NVLAP requires a cross-reference document mapping the lab’s management system to ISO/IEC 17025 clauses 4–8 and HB 150 annexes whenever numbering differs.
Common nonconformance: Org chart does not reflect actual reporting lines; scope claimed broader than witnessed methods.
§6 资源要求
Low · w 0.69
Maturity:14 linked docs · 26 FR-25 items
Body focus: A2LA P102 (instrument-specific metrological traceability) & P103 (uncertainty per discipline). NVLAP requires uncertainty budgets for ALL CMCs at application/renewal as source spreadsheets (not PDF-converted).
Common nonconformance: A2LA reports ~38% of its nonconformances under §6.4 (equipment cal records) and ~21% under §6.6 (externally provided products/services).
§7 过程要求
Medium · w 1
Maturity:29 linked docs · 46 FR-25 items
Body focus: A2LA R103 makes proficiency testing mandatory at initial/renewal/surveillance/annual review (PT plan F104). NVLAP requires a PT participation plan at renewal. Methods must be validated per field.
Common nonconformance: PT does not cover every accredited field; test report omits date of issue (§7.8.2.1 j); method performed outside approved scope.
§8 管理体系要求
Medium · w 0.814
Maturity:20 linked docs · 27 FR-25 items
Body focus: A2LA R102 conditions; internal audit (§8.8) + management review (§8.9) records required. NVLAP expects a fully implemented QMS before application; on-site every other year, certificate valid 1 year.
Common nonconformance: Management review outputs missing required records (§8.9.3); internal audit records incomplete.
