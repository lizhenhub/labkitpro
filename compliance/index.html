诚实的覆盖

若某机构的领域规则尚未结构化，工具会如实说明，并引导你上传官方文件。

共享上下文如何运作

You set country / accreditation body, lab type, stage, party and regulatory scheme once. Every tool (gap analysis, templates, compliance review, uncertainty) reads that same context and aligns its clauses, citations and depth to it. Nothing about your selection is sent to a model unless you choose to generate content; the alignment engine is a deterministic, edge-side function.
