Compliance at a glance

Six points that matter to certification bodies, accreditation bodies, auditors and your own legal review.

We cite, we don’t copy

Every output references standard numbers and official references only. No copyrighted standard text is reproduced or redistributed.

Your uploads never leave your device

Files you upload (templates, private docs, official rules) are processed in your browser only — never sent to our servers, never stored, never shared across users, never used for training.

No CAB decisions

LabKitPro produces technical drafts for your preparation. We do not issue, imply, or substitute for any certification or accreditation decision.

IP is respected

Our default templates are our copyrighted material; your uploaded documents remain yours. We claim no ownership of your content.

GDPR-aligned

Free tier stores nothing. Paid data is kept only as long as needed to deliver the service. Cookies are opt-in.

Honest coverage

Where a body’s field rules aren’t structured yet, the tool says so outright instead of implying coverage it does not have.

How the shared context works

You set country / accreditation body, lab type, stage, lab category once. Every tool (reality diagnosis, document generation, compliance review, uncertainty) reads that same context and aligns its clauses, citations and depth to it. Nothing about your selection is sent to a model unless you choose to generate content; the alignment engine is a deterministic, edge-side function.
