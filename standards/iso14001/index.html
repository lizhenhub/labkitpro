Current-edition maintenance self-check — ISO 14001 2015

  1. Keep your document inventory mapped to the current edition
  2. Run internal audits on schedule (at least annually)
  3. Hold management review at least once a year
  4. Monitor objectives & KPIs and act on unfavourable trends
  5. Close nonconformities with CAPA and verify effectiveness
  6. Prepare for the upcoming surveillance / recertification audit
