Frequently asked questions

Common questions about coverage, data safety, documents, uncertainty and billing. For anything else, the in-app AI assistant is happy to help.

What is LabKitPro?

LabKitPro is an AI workbench for ISO/IEC 17025 laboratory accreditation preparation. It helps you diagnose gaps, generate editable documents (Quality Manual, Procedures, Work Instructions, Forms/Records), run compliance reviews, and evaluate measurement uncertainty — in minutes, not weeks. We are software, not an accreditation body or consultancy.

What can I use for free vs paid?

Free vs paid, in detail: ① Free basic tier: every tool runs in your browser at no cost — the gap heat-map, document preview (watermarked), compliance screening and uncertainty budget all render on screen so you can try before you buy. ② Forms/Records are the exception: always free AND downloadable, watermark-free — never charged. ③ Paid (per delivery): one payment = one downloadable, context-aware file or report — country & accreditation body (Global ACI MRA general framework plus e.g. CNAS, A2LA, NVLAP, UKAS, NATA, SINGLAS, NABL), industry, lab stage and your own rules filled in, watermark removed. Regenerating for a new context or version is a new delivery — pay again or spend points. Per-item prices: Procedure / Work Instruction / single-element diagnosis / single-file review $1.9, Quality Manual / full review $9.9, Uncertainty $4.9. ④ Point packs: prepaid credit (1 point ≈ $1, packs include bonus points) — every generation/download deducts the item cost per delivery (2 points for a Procedure / Work Instruction / element diagnosis / single-file review, 5 for Uncertainty or the standards guide, 10 for a Quality Manual or full review); pack points expire 6 months after purchase. Ideal for medium or unpredictable volume. ⑤ Membership tiers — all one-time, never auto-renewed: Pro Monthly $29 (unlimited generation of every paid item, trial tier, Maintenance benefits not included); Maintenance Annual $99 (year-round compliance operations — calendar / audit-prep packs / CAPA tracking / document center — unlimited generation NOT included); Pro Annual $199 (all Maintenance benefits PLUS unlimited generation and batch export). Which accreditation standards and bodies do you cover?

We align to ISO/IEC 17025:2017 on the Global ACI mutual-recognition framework (which subsumed the former ILAC MRA on 1 January 2026) plus seven national bodies — CNAS (China), A2LA & NVLAP (USA), UKAS (UK), NATA (Australia), SINGLAS (Singapore) and NABL (India). The Global ACI general layer (17025 + Global ACI TECH-1-009, formerly ILAC P14) is built in for every lab; selecting a national body adds that country’s supplementary requirements. Every output carries the exact clause references (sourceRef) so you can verify.

What scope does LabKitPro cover?

This tool covers ISO/IEC 17025 testing & calibration only. Clinical and medical testing schemes fall outside the 17025 scope addressed here. For other industries, outputs are tailored to your selected sector — general / cross-discipline, calibration & metrology, mechanical & materials, automotive, electrical & electronic, chemical, biological / microbiological, food & agriculture, environmental, construction materials, textile/leather/consumer products, jewelry & precious metals, rubber & plastics, NDT, or software & IT — with the relevant accreditation-body focus points applied.

Do you replace the accreditation body or give accreditation?

No. LabKitPro is software only — we are not a conformity assessment body, a consultant, or a reviewer. We help you prepare; the accreditation decision always rests with your national body. Our outputs are advisory and your laboratory remains responsible for the final documents.

Is my data safe and GDPR-compliant?

Yes. The free tier stores nothing — results are generated in your browser. For paid users we keep only the account, order and entitlement records needed to deliver the service. Files you upload are processed in your browser and never sent to our servers, so there is nothing of that kind on our side to delete. Non-essential cookies are refused by default.

Can I use my own Word template / format?

Yes. In Document Generation you can upload your own .docx as a format shell. We read only its style structure (headings, fonts, header/footer, logo placeholders) and fill it with standards-driven content — all inside your browser. The file is never uploaded and never enters our public library.

What is your refund policy?

Because our products are digital files and reports generated on demand, all sales are final once the file or report is generated. Pro passes are one-time purchases and never auto-renew; the current paid period is not refunded.

How much does it cost?

Point packs (11/22/35 points, prepaid credit — every delivery deducts the item cost in points, e.g. 2 for a procedure, 10 for a manual) and membership tiers (one-time, never auto-renew: Pro Monthly $29, Maintenance Annual $99, Pro Annual $199 — see the pricing page) are also available. How deep is coverage for each country?

The Global ACI general layer is built in for every lab, and CNAS, A2LA, NVLAP, UKAS, NATA, SINGLAS and NABL are all fully structured and can be aligned directly from the built-in knowledge base. Selecting any national body adds its supplementary requirements on top of the Global ACI layer. Where a highly specialized local field document is not yet in the base, we say so outright — field-specific alignment against it is not available yet. We never claim depth we do not have.

Which uncertainty methods do you support?

Both the GUM method (JCGM 100:2008, incl. Amd.1:2026) and the Monte Carlo method (JCGM 101:2008). A guided default path lets beginners produce a compliant GUM budget (k=2, ≈95%) in minutes; advanced users can switch to MCM for non-linear or correlated models.

How fast can I get my documents?

Every tool runs free in your browser — the gap heat-map, compliance screening and uncertainty budget render instantly with no login. Paid documents and reports are generated in your browser immediately after checkout and can be downloaded right away.

What languages are supported?

The site is fully in English (en-US / en-GB / en-In). Generated documents follow the selected national annex; you may supply your own bilingual template if needed.

Can I add my own private standards or client requirements?

Not yet. Private standards and client requirements cannot be added automatically today — where a body’s field rules are not structured yet, the tool says so outright instead of implying coverage we do not have. Notes you type while using a tool stay in your browser; nothing you type or upload is sent to us or used for training.

Does Pro auto-renew? Can I cancel it?

No. Every tier is a one-time purchase — Pro Monthly $29, Maintenance Annual $99 or Pro Annual $199. It never auto-renews and there is nothing to cancel; when the period ends, simply buy again if you need more access.

How accurate is the compliance review?

The review compares your file against the selected country’s 17025 clauses and annexes, citing each basis. It is a preparation aid, not a certification. You — and your accreditation body — remain responsible for the final content. Always verify against the latest official edition.
