Frequently asked questions

Common questions about coverage, data safety, documents, uncertainty and billing. For anything else, the in-app AI assistant is happy to help.

What is LabKitPro?

LabKitPro is an AI workbench for ISO/IEC 17025 laboratory accreditation preparation. It helps you diagnose gaps, generate editable documents (Quality Manual, Procedures, Work Instructions, Forms/Records), run compliance reviews, and evaluate measurement uncertainty — in minutes, not weeks. We are software, not an accreditation body or consultancy.

What can I use for free vs paid?

Free vs paid, in detail: ① Free basic tier: every tool runs in your browser at no cost — the gap heat-map, document preview (watermarked), compliance screening and uncertainty budget all render on screen so you can try before you buy. ② Forms/Records are the exception: always free AND downloadable, watermark-free — never charged. ③ Paid (per item / per use): after payment you get the downloadable, context-aware file or report — country & accreditation body (e.g. CNAS, UKAS, A2LA), industry, lab stage and your own rules filled in, watermark removed. Per-item prices: Procedure / Work Instruction / single-element diagnosis / single-file review $1.99, Quality Manual / full review $9.99, Uncertainty $4.99. ④ Point packs: buy once, redeem per use (1 point ≈ $1), for medium-volume users. ⑤ Pro Membership: $29.99/mo, unlimited use of all four tools + batch export. ⑥ Refund: digital goods are final once generated — no per-item refund; Pro subscriptions can be cancelled anytime, no renewal, current period not refunded.

Which accreditation standards and bodies do you cover?

We align to ISO/IEC 17025:2017 and map national annexes for CNAS (China), A2LA & NVLAP (USA), UKAS (UK), NATA (Australia), SINGLAS (Singapore) and NABL (India). Every output carries the exact clause references (sourceRef) so you can verify.

Do you replace the accreditation body or give accreditation?

No. LabKitPro is software only — we are not a conformity assessment body, a consultant, or a reviewer. We help you prepare; the accreditation decision always rests with your national body. Our outputs are advisory and your laboratory remains responsible for the final documents.

Is my data safe and GDPR-compliant?

Yes. Free users store nothing. Paid users’ data is held in the EU region only for as long as needed to deliver the service. Uploaded custom materials are transient — auto-deleted within 24 hours, never cross-user, never used for training. Non-essential cookies are refused by default.

Can I use my own Word template / format?

Yes. In Document Generation you can upload your own .docx as a format shell. We read only its style structure (headings, fonts, header/footer, logo placeholders) and fill it with standards-driven content. The uploaded file is transient (≤24h) and never enters our public library.

What is your refund policy?

Because our products are digital files and reports generated on demand, all sales are final once the file or report is generated. Subscriptions can be cancelled anytime and will not renew; the current paid period is not refunded.

How much does it cost?

Forms/Records are free. Single procedures, work instructions and element diagnoses cost $1.99, a Quality Manual $9.99, a full compliance review $9.99, uncertainty evaluation $4.99. Point packs (11/22/35 points) and Pro membership ($29.99/mo, unlimited) are also available. No regional pricing — one global price.

How deep is coverage for each country?

CNAS, A2LA, NVLAP, NATA, UKAS, SINGLAS and NABL are all fully structured and can be aligned directly from the built-in knowledge base. Where a highly specialized local field document is not yet in the base, upload your licensed materials via the temporary channel and we align to them. We never claim depth we do not have.

Which uncertainty methods do you support?

Both the GUM method (JCGM 100:2008, incl. Amd.1:2026) and the Monte Carlo method (JCGM 101:2008). A guided default path lets beginners produce a compliant GUM budget (k=2, ≈95%) in minutes; advanced users can switch to MCM for non-linear or correlated models.

How fast can I get my documents?

Every tool runs free in your browser — the gap heat-map, compliance screening and uncertainty budget render instantly with no login. Paid documents and reports are generated in your browser immediately after checkout and can be downloaded right away.

What languages are supported?

The site is fully in English (en-US / en-GB / en-In). Generated documents follow the selected national annex; you may supply your own bilingual template if needed.

Can I add my own private standards or client requirements?

Yes, via the temporary material channel. Your custom rules participate in the current alignment and are purged within 24 hours. They are never shown to other users and never used for training.

Can I cancel my Pro subscription anytime?

Yes. Pro is month-to-month; cancel in one click and it will not renew. You keep access until the end of the paid period.

How accurate is the compliance review?

The review compares your file against the selected country’s 17025 clauses and annexes, citing each basis. It is a preparation aid, not a certification. You — and your accreditation body — remain responsible for the final content. Always verify against the latest official edition.

This product is designed to be self-serve. For product feedback or bug reports, email support@labkitpro.com. We read every message to improve the product.
