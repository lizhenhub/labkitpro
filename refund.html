Refund Policy

AI Video Summarizer · Last updated: November 1, 2026

The one-line principle

If we (or the payment processor) are at fault and you lost money, you get a full refund. If you tried Pro and simply changed your mind, the payment is non-refundable — but you can cancel at any time to stop all future charges.

When you can get a refund

The following are cases of fault on our side or the payment processor's side. Contact support@labkitpro.com within 14 days of the charge, and after verification we will refund the full amount to your original payment method:

CaseMeaning
Duplicate chargeThe same subscription plan was charged more than once — the extra charge is refunded in full
Charge after cancellationYou had already cancelled, but a charge still occurred
Technical errorA charge you did not personally confirm (for example, caused by a system fault)

When requesting a refund, please include: the email used at checkout, the order number (from your payment confirmation email), and a short description of the issue. We usually respond within 2–3 business days; refunds go back to the original payment method and typically arrive within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

When refunds are not available

Changed your mind after using the service · Forgot to cancel before renewal · Account banned for abuse — these three cases are non-refundable.

  • You changed your mind after using the service — you have already started using the current billing period. You can cancel anytime to stop future charges, but the current period is not refunded, partially or in full, for early cancellation.
  • Forgot to cancel before automatic renewal — you can cancel at any time before a renewal date; renewal charges that occur because the subscription was not cancelled are not refunded. Please cancel ahead of the renewal date.
  • Account banned for abuse — no refund for accounts terminated for violating our Terms (for example, sharing Pro access, abusing the free quota, or fraud). For accounts with repeated "purchase–refund" patterns or suspected fraud, we reserve the right to decline refunds, withdraw discounts, and ban the account.

Once a refund is processed, the corresponding Pro access ends immediately — it is not kept until the end of the billing period.

In special hardship cases, you may write to us and explain; we may, at our sole discretion, offer a refund or account credit. This discretion does not create an obligation. Where the law of your country grants you a statutory right of withdrawal (for example, EU/UK consumers generally have a 14-day withdrawal right for digital services under certain conditions), we fully comply with it.

How to cancel

You can cancel at any time. Cancelling means no further charges — it does not refund your current period.

  • Reply to your payment confirmation email and ask us to cancel, or
  • Email support@labkitpro.com

After cancelling, you keep Pro access until the end of the billing period you already paid for.

Yearly plans — cancelling mid-term

  • You keep Pro access until the end of the prepaid year.
  • Unused months are not refunded pro-rata.
  • Your subscription simply does not renew next year.

Chargebacks & disputes

If something went wrong, please email us first before opening a dispute with your bank or card company. Most issues are resolved with a single email. Going directly to a chargeback may take longer and cost you more, and may result in your account being suspended under our Terms.
