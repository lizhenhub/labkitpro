FAQ

Questions engineers actually ask

Is this aligned to the real standards, not AI-invented content?

Yes. The baseline is ISO/IEC 17025:2017, with ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018 mapped to the same clause architecture. Every checklist item cites its clause.

We run 9001, 14001 and 45001 together. Do I answer everything three times?

No — that is the point of the shared context. Common clauses (context, leadership, risk, internal audit, management review) are answered once and reused across all three standards, including the integrated manual.

Does this replace our certification body or consultant?

No. LabKitPro prepares you for both — it gets your documents and readiness to the level where the auditor's job is confirmation, not discovery.

What do the free tools actually cost?

Diagnosis and all on-page calculators are free with example data. You pay one-time prices only when you export a full report or document — or go Pro for unlimited generation.
