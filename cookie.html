Cookie Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026 · Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy explains how LabKitPro uses cookies and similar technologies.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. LabKitPro uses a minimal set: a consent record and, when you use the Service, local storage entries that remember your inputs on this device.

2. Categories we use

  • Essential — required for the Service to function (e.g. remembering your cookie choice and saved work on this device). These are always on.
  • Analytics (non-essential) — aggregated, non-identifiable usage statistics that help us improve the Service. These load only after you accept via the consent banner and can be rejected without affecting core functionality.

3. Managing your choice

When you first visit, a banner asks you to accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site's storage in your browser settings, after which the banner will reappear. Rejecting non-essential cookies does not disable the Service.

4. Contact

Questions: support@labkitpro.com.
