ISO/IEC 17025 vs ISO 9001

Scope, technical competence, and why laboratories need 17025 — not just 9001.

The core difference

DimensionISO 9001ISO/IEC 17025
ScopeAny organizationTesting & calibration labs
Technical competenceNot coveredRequired (clauses 6–7)
Uncertainty & traceabilityNot requiredMandatory (§7.6, §6.5)
AccreditationCertification (optional)Accreditation by NABL/A2LA/UKAS etc.

Why labs need 17025

Customers and regulators that require accredited results reference ISO/IEC 17025:2017 specifically. A 9001 certificate signals good quality practice but does not prove your measurements are technically valid.
