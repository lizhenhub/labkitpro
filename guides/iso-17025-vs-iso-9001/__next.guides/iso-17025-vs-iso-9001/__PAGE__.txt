<h2 style="font-size:19px;margin-top:24px;">Why labs need 17025</h2>
<p>Customers and regulators that require <em>accredited</em> results reference ISO/IEC 17025:2017 specifically. A 9001 certificate signals good quality practice but does not prove your measurements are technically valid.</p>
