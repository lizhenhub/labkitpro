<h1 style="font-size:27px;margin:14px 0 6px;">ILAC Document Templates for ISO/IEC 17025 (Global MRA)</h1>
<p style="color:#5b6675;">Documents for labs whose reports must carry ILAC MRA recognition — without the consultant retainer.</p>

<h2 style="font-size:19px;margin-top:24px;">ILAC and the standard</h2>
<p>ILAC (International Laboratory Accreditation Cooperation) is not an accreditation body — it is the <strong>global Mutual Recognition Arrangement (MRA)</strong> that makes your accreditation portable across borders. Your national body (CNAS, A2LA, UKAS, NVLAP, NATA, SINGLAS, NABL and others) assesses you against <strong>ISO/IEC 17025:2017</strong>; ILAC MRA signatory status is what lets customers and regulators in other countries accept your reports.</p>
<p>For laboratories, the ILAC documents that matter most are the <strong>P-series policies</strong>: <strong>ILAC P8</strong> (use of the ILAC MRA mark and combined marks on reports), <strong>ILAC R7</strong> (joint use of accreditation symbols with other bodies), <strong>ILAC P9</strong> (proficiency testing policy and frequencies), and <strong>ILAC P10</strong> (metrological traceability policy). Your accreditation body enforces these through its own criteria, so your management system must show how reports, marks and PT results are controlled.</p>

<h2 style="font-size:19px;margin-top:24px;">The core document set</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Quality Manual</strong> — policy, scope, and the documented structure of your ISO/IEC 17025 system.</li>
  <li><strong>Report &amp; Mark Control Procedure</strong> — how the ILAC MRA symbol and your body's mark are applied, and when they must be withheld (suspension, out-of-scope work, endorsed vs non-endorsed results).</li>
  <li><strong>Proficiency Testing Plan</strong> — participation mapped to ILAC P9 frequencies and your body's PT policy.</li>
  <li><strong>Traceability Procedure</strong> — metrological traceability statements consistent with ILAC P10.</li>
  <li><strong>Uncertainty of Measurement</strong> — a GUM or Monte Carlo budget per measurand, supporting decision-rule statements on reports.</li>
</ul>
