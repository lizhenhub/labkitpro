ISO/IEC 17025 Internal Audit Checklist

Clause-by-clause self-review mapped to your accreditation body — find gaps before the assessor does.

The five clause blocks

  • §4 General requirements — impartiality, confidentiality.
  • §5 Structural requirements — legal identity, organizational structure.
  • §6 Resource requirements — personnel, equipment, metrological traceability.
  • §7 Process requirements — review of requests, method validation, sampling, handling, uncertainty.
  • §8 Management system — documentation, control, internal audit, management review, improvement.
