<h2 style="font-size:19px;margin-top:24px;">Where GLP is different</h2>
<p><strong>GLP</strong> is not an accreditation body — it is a quality system (OECD Principles) for non-clinical <em>study conduct</em>, distinct from 17025's testing/calibration competence.
