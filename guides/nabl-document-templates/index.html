What NABL expects

NABL (National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories) assesses Indian labs against ISO/IEC 17025:2017. The assessor's job is to verify that your management system produces objective evidence for every clause from §4 (General) through §8 (Management system). The documentation backbone is the same across labs, but the content must reflect your specific scope, equipment, and methods.

The core document set

  • Quality Manual — policy, scope, and the documented structure of your system.
  • Document & Record Control Procedures — how versions are approved, issued, and retained.
  • Internal Audit & Management Review Procedures — your self-checking loop.
  • Method / Calibration Procedures — how measurements are actually performed and validated.
  • Uncertainty of Measurement — a GUM or Monte Carlo budget per measurand.
