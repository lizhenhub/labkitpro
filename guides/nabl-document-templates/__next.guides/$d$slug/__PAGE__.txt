<h2 style="font-size:19px;margin-top:24px;">What NABL expects</h2>
<p>NABL (National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories) assesses Indian labs against <strong>ISO/IEC 17025:2017</strong>. The assessor's job is to verify that your management system produces <em>objective evidence</em> for every clause from §4 (General) through §8 (Management system). The documentation backbone is the same across labs, but the <em>content</em> must reflect your specific scope, equipment, and methods.</p>

<h2 style="font-size:19px;margin-top:24px;">The core document set</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Quality Manual</strong> — policy, scope, and the documented structure of your system.</li>
  <li><strong>Document &amp; Record Control Procedures</strong> — how versions are approved, issued, and retained.</li>
  <li><strong>Internal Audit &amp; Management Review Procedures</strong> — your self-checking loop.</li>
  <li><strong>Method / Calibration Procedures</strong> — how measurements are actually performed and validated.</li>
  <li><strong>Uncertainty of Measurement</strong> — a GUM or Monte Carlo budget per measurand.</li>
</ul>
