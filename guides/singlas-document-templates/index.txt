<h2 style="font-size:19px;margin-top:24px;">SINGLAS and the standard</h2>
<p>SINGLAS (Singapore Laboratory Accreditation Scheme) is operated under the <strong>Singapore Accreditation Council (SAC)</strong>, administered by <strong>Enterprise Singapore</strong>. SINGLAS accredits testing and calibration laboratories against <strong>ISO/IEC 17025:2017</strong> together with SAC-SINGLAS specific criteria and technical guides. Areas that trip up Singapore labs in assessment include <strong>SAC-SINGLAS mark usage conditions</strong>, <strong>conformity-statement (decision rule) wording</strong> on reports, <strong>subcontracting controls</strong> (only to accredited or approved providers, with responsibility retained), and <strong>proficiency testing participation</strong> consistent with ILAC P9. Walk through these before your assessment.</p>

<h2 style="font-size:19px;margin-top:24px;">The core document set</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Quality Manual</strong> — policy, scope, and the documented structure of your ISO/IEC 17025 system.</li>
  <li><strong>Report &amp; Mark Control Procedure</strong> — SAC-SINGLAS mark conditions, endorsed results, and decision-rule statements on reports.</li>
  <li><strong>Subcontracting Procedure</strong> — provider qualification, accreditation checks, and retained responsibility for subcontracted work.</li>
  <li><strong>Proficiency Testing Plan</strong> — participation mapped to ILAC P9 frequencies and SAC policy.</li>
  <li><strong>Uncertainty of Measurement</strong> — a GUM or Monte Carlo budget per measurand.</li>
</ul>
