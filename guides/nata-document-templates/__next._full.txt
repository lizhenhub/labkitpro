<h2 style="font-size:19px;margin-top:24px;">NATA and the standard</h2>
<p>NATA (National Association of Testing Authorities, Australia) accredits laboratories across the region to <strong>ISO/IEC 17025:2017</strong>. NATA's own criteria (such as MR001) operationalise the 2017 edition for Australian scopes; <strong>SINGLAS</strong> plays the equivalent accreditation role in Singapore. The assessor's job is to confirm objective evidence for every clause from §4 through §8.</p>

<h2 style="font-size:19px;margin-top:24px;">The core document set</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Quality Manual</strong> — policy, scope, and the documented structure of your system.</li>
  <li><strong>Document &amp; Record Control Procedures</strong> — version approval, issue, and retention.</li>
  <li><strong>Internal Audit &amp; Management Review Procedures</strong> — your self-checking loop.</li>
  <li><strong>Method / Calibration Procedures</strong> — how measurements are performed and validated.</li>
  <li><strong>Uncertainty of Measurement</strong> — a GUM or Monte Carlo budget per measurand.</li>
</ul>
