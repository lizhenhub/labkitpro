NATA and the standard

NATA (National Association of Testing Authorities, Australia) accredits laboratories across the region to ISO/IEC 17025:2017. NATA's own criteria (such as MR001) operationalise the 2017 edition for Australian scopes; SINGLAS plays the equivalent accreditation role in Singapore. The assessor's job is to confirm objective evidence for every clause from §4 through §8.

The core document set

  • Quality Manual — policy, scope, and the documented structure of your system.
  • Document & Record Control Procedures — version approval, issue, and retention.
  • Internal Audit & Management Review Procedures — your self-checking loop.
  • Method / Calibration Procedures — how measurements are performed and validated.
  • Uncertainty of Measurement — a GUM or Monte Carlo budget per measurand.
