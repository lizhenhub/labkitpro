<h1 style="font-size:27px;margin:14px 0 6px;">ISO/IEC 17025 Measurement Uncertainty Budget</h1>
<p style="color:#5b6675;">GUM bottom-up and Monte Carlo — generate a clause-ready uncertainty evaluation in minutes.</p>

<h2 style="font-size:19px;margin-top:24px;">Why it matters</h2>
<p>Clause 7.6 of <strong>ISO/IEC 17025:2017</strong> requires every lab to identify the contributions to uncertainty and to use a valid procedure for estimation. Assessors routinely flag missing or superficial budgets as a major nonconformity.</p>

<h2 style="font-size:19px;margin-top:24px;">Two accepted methods</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>GUM (bottom-up):</strong> list input quantities, assign standard uncertainties, combine via the law of propagation of uncertainty, expand by a coverage factor <em>k</em>.</li>
  <li><strong>Monte Carlo:</strong> propagate a defined probability distribution through the measurement model to approximate the output distribution — useful for non-linear or correlated models.</li>
</ul>
