ISO/IEC 17025 Measurement Uncertainty Budget

GUM bottom-up and Monte Carlo — generate a clause-ready uncertainty evaluation in minutes.

Why it matters

Clause 7.6 of ISO/IEC 17025:2017 requires every lab to identify the contributions to uncertainty and to use a valid procedure for estimation. Assessors routinely flag missing or superficial budgets as a major nonconformity.

Two accepted methods

  • GUM (bottom-up): list input quantities, assign standard uncertainties, combine via the law of propagation of uncertainty, expand by a coverage factor k.
  • Monte Carlo: propagate a defined probability distribution through the measurement model to approximate the output distribution — useful for non-linear or correlated models.
