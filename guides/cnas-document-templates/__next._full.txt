<h2 style="font-size:19px;margin-top:24px;">CNAS and the standard</h2>
<p>CNAS (China National Accreditation Service for Conformity Assessment) is China's national accreditation body. Its laboratory criteria <strong>CNAS-CL01:2018</strong> adopt <strong>ISO/IEC 17025:2017</strong> (the current, third edition). A CNAS-accredited lab therefore demonstrates compliance with the 2017 edition of the standard — not any "2025 edition", which does not exist. The assessor verifies that your management system produces <em>objective evidence</em> for every clause from §4 (General) through §8 (Management system).</p>

<h2 style="font-size:19px;margin-top:24px;">The core document set</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Quality Manual (质量手册)</strong> — policy, scope, and the documented structure of your system.</li>
  <li><strong>Document &amp; Record Control Procedures (文件与记录控制)</strong> — how versions are approved, issued, and retained.</li>
  <li><strong>Internal Audit &amp; Management Review Procedures (内部审核与管理评审)</strong> — your self-checking loop.</li>
  <li><strong>Method / Calibration Procedures (方法/校准)</strong> — how measurements are performed and validated.</li>
  <li><strong>Uncertainty of Measurement (测量不确定度)</strong> — a GUM or Monte Carlo budget per measurand.</li>
</ul>
