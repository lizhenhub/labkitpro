<h2 style="font-size:19px;margin-top:24px;">UKAS and 17025</h2>
<p>UKAS (United Kingdom Accreditation Service) is the national accreditation body and an ILAC signatory. UK labs seeking accreditation must demonstrate a management system satisfying <strong>ISO/IEC 17025:2017</strong> clauses §4–§8, with documentation that an assessor can audit clause-by-clause.</p>
