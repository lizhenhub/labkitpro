NVLAP and the standard

NVLAP (National Voluntary Laboratory Accreditation Program) is administered by NIST, the US national metrology institute. NVLAP accredits laboratories against ISO/IEC 17025:2017 operationalized through the NIST Handbook 150 series: Handbook 150 sets the general procedures and criteria, and each program-specific handbook (such as the Calibration Laboratories Technical Guide) adds technical requirements for your field. NVLAP places particular weight on metrological traceability to SI/NIST, proficiency testing (interlaboratory comparisons), certificate and logo use, and the annual on-site assessment cycle — calibration laboratories should pay special attention to these.

The core document set

  • Quality Manual — policy, scope, and the documented structure of your ISO/IEC 17025 system.
  • Traceability Procedure — unbroken calibration chains to SI via NIST, with uncertainty at each step.
  • Proficiency Testing Plan — interlaboratory comparison participation per NVLAP policy and your program handbook.
  • Certificate & Logo Control Procedure — how the NVLAP logo and your accredited scope appear on reports and certificates, and when they must be withheld.
  • Uncertainty of Measurement — a GUM or Monte Carlo budget per measurand, with valid CMC claims.
