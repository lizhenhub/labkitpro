<h2 style="font-size:19px;margin-top:24px;">NVLAP and the standard</h2>
<p>NVLAP (National Voluntary Laboratory Accreditation Program) is administered by <strong>NIST</strong>, the US national metrology institute. NVLAP accredits laboratories against <strong>ISO/IEC 17025:2017</strong> operationalized through the <strong>NIST Handbook 150</strong> series: Handbook 150 sets the general procedures and criteria, and each program-specific handbook (such as the Calibration Laboratories Technical Guide) adds technical requirements for your field. NVLAP places particular weight on <strong>metrological traceability to SI/NIST</strong>, <strong>proficiency testing (interlaboratory comparisons)</strong>, <strong>certificate and logo use</strong>, and the annual on-site assessment cycle — calibration laboratories should pay special attention to these.</p>

<h2 style="font-size:19px;margin-top:24px;">The core document set</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Quality Manual</strong> — policy, scope, and the documented structure of your ISO/IEC 17025 system.</li>
  <li><strong>Traceability Procedure</strong> — unbroken calibration chains to SI via NIST, with uncertainty at each step.</li>
  <li><strong>Proficiency Testing Plan</strong> — interlaboratory comparison participation per NVLAP policy and your program handbook.</li>
  <li><strong>Certificate &amp; Logo Control Procedure</strong> — how the NVLAP logo and your accredited scope appear on reports and certificates, and when they must be withheld.</li>
  <li><strong>Uncertainty of Measurement</strong> — a GUM or Monte Carlo budget per measurand, with valid CMC claims.</li>
</ul>
