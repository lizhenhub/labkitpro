ISO 45001:2027 (expected): DIS stage, timeline & how to prepare

ISO 45001:2027 is currently at the DIS (Draft International Standard) stage; the DIS vote closed on 9 September 2026 and ISO expects publication in the first half of 2027. The text is not yet final — this page is an early look and preparation guide only; everything is subject to the official release.

What this revision changes

Following ISO’s management-system revision practice, 45001:2027 is expected to adopt the refreshed Annex SL structure and align with the 2026 editions of 9001/14001. Specific clause changes will be added here once published.

45001 is still in draft, so we do not sell any '2027 transition pack' here — there is no authorized content to deliver before release. Instead, start your 9001/14001 transition now (Annex SL alignment means much carries over) and use the free compliance calendar to track the 45001:2027 publication.

  2. 2Run a gap analysis against the 2026 edition
  3. 3Update documentation (manual, procedures, risk/opportunity register)
  4. 4Train staff, run an internal audit and management review
  5. 5Book the transition audit before the cutoff

Frequently asked questions

What is the status of ISO 45001:2027 now?

It is at the DIS (Draft International Standard) stage; the DIS vote closed on 9 September 2026 and ISO expects publication in the first half of 2027. The text is not yet final.

How long will the ISO 45001:2027 transition period be?

The transition period is not yet announced. Based on ISO’s typical ~3-year transition it would be expected around 2030, but this is indicative only — the official Global ACI timeline governs.

Should I wait for ISO 45001:2027 now?

No need to wait. Start your 9001:2026 and 14001:2026 transitions now (shared Annex SL means much carries over), and use the free compliance calendar to be alerted when 45001:2027 is published.

What will change in ISO 45001:2027?

The text is not final, so no confirmed clause changes yet. It is expected to adopt the refreshed Annex SL and align with the 2026 management-system revisions. This page will be updated on release.
