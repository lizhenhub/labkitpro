ILAC Mutual Recognition Documents · By-Sector Classification

This list groups the current ILAC public documents by “official document series + guidance theme + field-specific guidance”, corresponding item by item and identical in count to the full-list volume “ILAC Mutual Recognition Documents (Full List)”. Note: ILAC is the coordination organisation for international mutual recognition arrangements; its document system centres on MRA policies, accreditation rules and general guidance, and has no field-segmentation by testing industry (e.g. food, environment, construction). Only a small number of field-specific guides exist within the G series (racing / anti-doping, forensic science, medical, inspection bodies). Group names and assignments here map directly to the official series columns and document titles — no field categories are invented.

Grouping note: the ILAC official website organises documents only by the five series P (policy), G (guidance), R (rules), A (IAF/ILAC joint assessment) and B (publicity) — it does not segment by testing industry. This list follows that structure: P / R / A / B each form one group; the G series is split into “general themes” and “field-specific” — the field-specific group contains only G7 (racing laboratories), G19 (forensic science), G26 (medical laboratories), G27/G28 (inspection bodies) and G29 (anti-doping laboratories), six documents in total; the remaining G3/G8/G17/G18/G21/G24 are general guidance. Each current document is assigned to one primary group; official links for each document are in the companion full-list volume.

Data source & notes

This list is compiled from the ILAC archive site (ilac.org) public columns (verified 2026-10-19): the Publications List master list and the P/G/R/A/B / proposed / withdrawn series pages. ILAC has no industry-segmented document system; grouping follows the official series and guidance themes. Official links for each document are in the companion full-list volume “ILAC Mutual Recognition Documents (Full List)”.

Usage note

This classification has 63 entries, corresponding one-to-one and identical in count to the full-list volume; only current documents are listed — withdrawn / superseded versions (G9, G11, P11, P13, A4, A5, A6, R2, and the A1/A2 remote-assessment annexes) are covered by the corresponding note-lines in the full-list volume. Subject to the official current versions.
