SINGLAS — Current Documents of the Singapore Laboratory Accreditation Scheme

SINGLAS (Singapore Laboratory Accreditation Scheme), administered by the Singapore Accreditation Council (SAC), covers testing / calibration laboratories (ISO/IEC 17025:2017) and medical testing laboratories (ISO 15189:2022). Compiled from the SAC website (SAC Documents → Laboratory Accreditation), this list includes all-field publications, core documents on accreditation workflow and traceability, and field-specific requirements, guides and checklists (calibration & measurement, chemical / biological / environmental, civil, electrical, functional food, IT, mechanical, NDT, medical), with current editions and dates. Note: from 2026-01-01 the former ILAC and IAF merged into the Global Accreditation Cooperation Incorporated (Global ACI); the former ILAC MRA continues in force. Document titles without an official English edition are shown in Chinese.
