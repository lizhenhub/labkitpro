CNAS · Specific Requirements Beyond ISO/IEC 17025:2017

CNAS · Specific Requirements Relative to ISO/IEC 17025:2017 — Explained

CNAS (China National Accreditation Service for Conformity Assessment) is the sole laboratory accreditation body in mainland China. The core relationship: CNAS-CL01:2018 is an identical adoption of ISO/IEC 17025:2017; on top of it sit the CL01-G001 application requirements (mandatory) and the CL01-A series of field-specific application notes (26 testing/calibration fields), forming a layered document system of General Rules (R/RL) + Basic Criteria (CL) + Application Requirements (G) + Field Application Notes (A) + Guidance (GL) + Explanatory Notes (EL) + Technical Reports (TRL).

!Key Points

Of the 45 files distilled across five areas, the core combination CNAS-system laboratories must put in place first is CNAS-RL01 (Rules for Accreditation of Laboratories) + CNAS-CL01:2018 (identical adoption of ISO/IEC 17025:2017) + CL01-G001–G004 (the mandatory application-requirements "four-piece set"), then lock the CL01-A application note(s) matching their industry; the rest are supporting guidance or on-demand documents.

1

Relationship to ISO/IEC 17025:2017

Identical adoption · layered application requirements

CNAS adopts ISO/IEC 17025:2017 identically (CNAS-CL01:2018), but accreditation practice goes beyond the criteria itself: all testing/calibration laboratories must also meet the G-series application requirements and the A-series application notes for their industry — this is the core form of CNAS's "additional requirements" beyond 17025.

Layered document system

Layer 1 · General rules (R/RL)R01–R03 (marks / impartiality / appeals & complaints) + RL01–RL10 rules on laboratory accreditation, proficiency testing, fees, acceptance of overseas bodies, etc.
Layer 2 · Basic criteria (CL01–CL10)CL01 identical to ISO/IEC 17025:2017; CL02 identical to ISO 15189:2022 (medical), etc., selected by organization type
Layer 3 · Application requirements (G series)G001 application requirements (mandatory) + G002 metrological traceability / G003 uncertainty / G004 internal calibration
Layer 4 · Field application notes (A series)CL01-A series for 26 testing/calibration fields + the A series of CL02–CL08 dedicated criteria (medical / forensic, etc.)
Layer 5 · Guidance & reports (GL/EL/S/TRL)Implementation guidance, acceptance and scope-expression notes, dedicated accreditation schemes (ENERGY STAR / Bluetooth / OIML, etc.), technical reports

General Rules / Additional Accreditation Conditions

All applicants

The R/RL series are the hard rules governing the whole CNAS accreditation process: mark use, impartiality, appeals and complaints, proficiency testing, fees, acceptance of overseas bodies — 17025 only governs laboratory competence; CNAS uses R/RL to govern "the act of accreditation itself".

Sector-Specific Requirements

Choose the A series by industry · choose schemes by business

CNAS's most distinctive additional mechanism beyond 17025: the CL01-A series issues application notes field by field across 26 testing/calibration fields — you must lock your industry's note(s) before applying. In addition, dedicated criteria for medical and forensic institutions (CL02/CL08, identical adoptions of other international standards) and dedicated accreditation schemes (S series) are selected by business need.

Additional Policies on Metrological Traceability · Measurement Uncertainty · Proficiency Testing

G series mandatory · always checked in assessments

17025:2017 states the principles for traceability / uncertainty / PT; CNAS gives the execution requirements through the G series — G001 is the master application requirement (mandatory), and G002/G003/G004 are the dedicated requirements for traceability, uncertainty and internal calibration, implemented with GL guidance.

Dense 2025–2026 revisions

CNAS revisions/new releases are dense in 2025–2026 (R03, RL10, A004, A018, the TRL series, etc.). For accredited laboratories, the maintenance focus is: accept surveillance assessments per RL01, continuously satisfy RL02 proficiency testing, and track versions of the A and G series.

Practical Points & Common Pitfalls

6 tips

The CNAS document system is the most complete — and the easiest to get lost in. The 6 points below are the most common pitfalls in application and maintenance.

1First step: lock the CL01-A series by industry

First check the industry classification list to determine the A-series application note(s) for your industry (26 fields), then fill in the technical details per the A document.Choosing wrong/missing A notes is the most common reason applications are sent back

2G001–G004 is the mandatory "four-piece set"

G001 application requirements, G002 traceability, G003 uncertainty, G004 internal calibration are mandatory for testing/calibration laboratories; missing any one draws a nonconformity in assessment.Don't stare only at the CL01 text — CNAS's additional positions all live in the G series

3Schedule proficiency testing early per RL02

PT must be participated in annually by field and meet frequency requirements; annual PT compliance is the stage most likely to go wrong before assessment.Keep records of PT participation and of handling unsatisfactory results

4Two core application documents: EL-15 + EL-03

Acceptance conditions and materials per EL-15; scope filing per EL-03 (2023-edition wording); a wrong scope-table format means rework.Report/certificate format follows EL-13 separately

5Dense 2026 revisions — check the version before acting

R03:2026, A004:2026 (2018 ed. withdrawn 2026-10-31), A018:2026 (effective 2027-1-1), RL10:2026, new TRL series of 2026 — verify the current version on the official website before implementing.Preparing an assessment with an outdated A document is a high-frequency error during revision periods

6Select the A series of dedicated criteria separately from CL01-A

Medical (CL02), forensic (CL08), PTP (CL03) and other type-specific criteria each have their own A series — don't mix them with CL01-A.Different organization types mean different criteria and A documents
