ILAC Common-Layer Document List (P / G / R / joint IAF-ILAC A series, full edition)

This list covers the current public ILAC document system: MRA policy documents (P series), accreditation guidance (G series), organisational rules (R series), joint IAF/ILAC assessment documents (A series), proposed documents and B-series public promotional material. Important transition: ILAC ceased operations on 2026-01-01 and began dissolution; ilac.org is now an archival/legacy site, and its functions have been taken over by Global ACI (Global Accreditation Cooperation Incorporated). Under Global ACI resolutions 2025-25 and 2025-29, designated ILAC P-series, applicable A-series and R6/R7/P8/G21 documents remain valid until the corresponding Global ACI documents are published; individual documents have already been superseded — each row is annotated accordingly.

How to use

ILAC and IAF merged into Global ACI on 2026-01-01; the ILAC document system is in transition. Designated documents remain valid per resolution, some have been superseded by Global ACI-GOV / MS / MRA / TECH numbered documents, and a few are still in development or migration. The current official edition always governs.

Resources on this page are compiled from publicly published accreditation-body documents (references, titles and paraphrases only — no verbatim text), for internal preparation and study only; they do not constitute accreditation-body decisions or legal advice.
