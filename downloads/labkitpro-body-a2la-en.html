A2LA · Specific Requirements Relative to ISO/IEC 17025:2017 — Explained

A2LA (American Association for Laboratory Accreditation) is the largest non-governmental laboratory accreditation body in the United States. How it works: it transposes ISO/IEC 17025:2017 via R101 "General Requirements", then overlays program-specific R2xx requirements, P-series policies (traceability / uncertainty / flexible scopes, etc.), G-series guidance and F-series scope selection lists — a four-piece set: "R101 transposition + sector R2xx + P/G policies & guidance + F-series application interface".

!Key Points

Of the 42 files distilled across five areas, the core document combination a laboratory must put in place first is R101 (transposition of 17025) + R102 (Conditions for Accreditation) + R103 (Proficiency Testing) + R105 (Referencing Accredited Status) + P102 (Metrological Traceability) + P103 (Uncertainty in Testing, choose the a–e annex by sector); sector additions follow the R2xx of the program you apply for, and the application interface is the F-series scope selection lists.

1

Relationship to ISO/IEC 17025:2017

R101 transposition · sector R2xx

A2LA does not simply "identically adopt" 17025; instead it uses its own R101 general requirements to transpose the clauses of 17025:2017 into A2LA's accreditation language, then overlays program-specific R2xx requirements by sector. Traceability and uncertainty are additionally mandated by P102/P103 — this is the full shape of A2LA's additional requirements beyond 17025.

Layered document system

Layer 1 · International baselineISO/IEC 17025:2017 (testing/calibration), ISO 15189:2022 (medical), ISO/IEC 17043, ISO 17034, ISO 20387 (biobanking)
Layer 2 · General requirements (R1xx)R101 transposes 17025 + R102 conditions for accreditation + R103 PT general requirements + R105 referencing accredited status
Layer 3 · Sector-specific requirements (R2xx)Construction materials / geotechnical / NDT / forensics / electrical / legal metrology / special inspection agencies / FedRAMP / medical (R602), etc.
Layer 4 · Policy documents (P series)P102 traceability, P103 uncertainty (+P103a–e sector annexes / P903 medical), flexible scopes, relocation, satellite laboratories, remote assessment
Layer 5 · Guidance & forms (G/F series)G-series implementation guidance (uncertainty / scopes / decision rules) + F-series scope-of-accreditation selection lists (the application filing interface)

General Rules / Additional Accreditation Conditions

R/P series · applies to all

Practical Points & Common Pitfalls

6 tips

The A2LA four-piece set (R/P/G/F) each has its role; the most common pitfalls concentrate on "choosing the right documents by sector".

1R101 is the "transposition vehicle"; assessments check R101 + R2xx together

R101 turns all 17025 clauses into A2LA requirements, with sector additions in R2xx — have both on hand when preparing for assessment.Reading only the 17025 original misses A2LA's reworded baseline

2Choose the uncertainty annex by sector

P103 main policy + P103a–e sector annexes (automotive materials / life sciences / EMC / construction materials & geotechnical / forensics) by business; medical follows P903.Choosing the wrong annex = a wrong uncertainty scheme

3Apply with the F-series scope selection lists

Choose the right F201–F344 list by sector (calibration F204 / chemical F205 / construction materials F208 / electrical F209…), together with the F117 staff matrix.The scope list is the only interface for declaring capability

4Consensus decisions are sector execution baselines

P108/P111/P115/P116 (advisory committee technical consensus decisions) may be cited in assessment — bring them into your system documents when the relevant sector applies.Consensus decisions are updated frequently — include them in version tracking

5Dense revisions in 2025–2026 — check the version before executing

R101/R102/R103, the P103 series, P105/P112/P120/P121 have all been revised/added — verify issue dates on the website before use.Using outdated policies against an assessment is a common error during the revision period

6Medical laboratories run a dual track — don't mix with 17025

Medical testing laboratories follow R602/R603 (ISO 15189 + CLIA) + P903 uncertainty, separate from the 17025 document system.Mixing the two tracks will be found non-compliant
