SINGLAS Accreditation Documents · By-Sector Classification

This list groups current SINGLAS documents under the accreditation fields / programmes that actually exist on the SAC website (general management & cross-field framework, plus 9 specialist fields: calibration & measurement, chemical-biological-environmental, civil engineering, electrical, functional food, information technology, mechanical, non-destructive testing, medical testing). Each current document is assigned to one primary group; cross-cutting shared documents state “also applies to …” in the group note-line. All group names come from official SAC columns (Calibration and Measurement / Chemical & Biological / Environmental / Civil Engineering / Electrical / Functional Food / Information Technology / Mechanical / Non-Destructive / Medical Testing Field) and are not invented.
