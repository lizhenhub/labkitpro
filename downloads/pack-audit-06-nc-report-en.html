5　Good / Poor Wording Reference

Poor fact statement: “Section 7.8 not conforming, report quality problems” — no facts, cannot be disputed, cannot be corrected.

Good fact statement: “Report No. TR-2026-031, issued 2026-02-18, does not state the sampling location required by clause 7.8.3; the sampling record shows the site was A001, and the template revision TR-2025-02 in use also omits this field.” — reproducible, citable, fixable.

Handling rules: ① facts confirmed with the auditee on site, disagreement recorded verbatim; ② major NCs additionally assess the impact on previously issued results immediately (§7.10); ③ correction evidence due within 15 working days; verification closes the loop and feeds IA-06; ④ retain under record control no less than 6 years.
