Internal Audit Checklist (Full Element, Itemised)

Covers every clause of ISO/IEC 17025:2017 §4.1–§8.9, with each clause broken down into executable audit items: what to check / how to verify / conformity criteria (what good looks like & red lines) / evidence record / judgement. Use together with IA-WI-01.
Form No. IA-04 Version V2.0 (itemised upgrade) §4 General · §5 Structure · §6 Resources · §7 Process · §8 Management Same source as website library FR-40
How to use: ① Each audit activates only the clauses in scope — avoid “one sheet for every audit”; ② each audit item is a discrete on-site action: the auditor must actually carry out the check and record evidence and judgement — no whole-row ticking; ③ sample at least 3 records or 1 on-site observation per item; ④ Judgement: √ = conforming, × = nonconforming (raise FR-24, stating the clause and facts), ○ = observation / opportunity for improvement; ⑤ the evidence column must record specific document numbers / record numbers / quantities so findings remain traceable — this column feeds FR-25.
√ Conforming× Nonconforming (raise FR-24)○ ObservationEvidence examples: Record FR-24-2026-003 / Calibration certificate No. AB-2207 / Interview with [Name]

§4General RequirementsImpartiality · Confidentiality

This block examines whether the laboratory is genuinely independent of conflicts of interest and protects customer information boundaries. Focus on “demonstrable commitments” and “actions assigned to positions” — not slogans on the wall.
ItemAudit item (what to check)Verification method (how to check)Conformity criteria (good / red line)Evidence recordJudgement
4.1.1-①Impartiality commitment signedCheck that top management and key roles (technical/quality/business managers) have signed written impartiality commitments covering testing, calibration and opinions and interpretationsGood: signed and dated, covering all key roles including front-line staff.Red line: only management signed, front-line testers did not; commitment excludes opinions and interpretations　　
4.1.1-②Free from undue pressureInterview 2–3 staff to confirm awareness that commercial or financial pressures must not affect results; check for records of pressure from sales/customers to alter resultsGood: staff can accurately state the independence requirement.Red line: staff say “we would accommodate a customer asking to change a result”　　
4.1.1-③Conflict-of-interest separationCheck for organisational arrangements that both produce and test in the same field; if present, check the parent-body authorisation for business/testing separation and reporting linesGood: the testing line reports independently to the Quality Manager, not under sales.Red line: testing performance is linked to sales commissions　　
4.1.2-①Policy as a demonstrable commitmentCheck the impartiality policy clause in the Quality Manual; check communication records (training sign-in / intranet notice) that the policy reached the positionsGood: policy documented + communication traceable.Red line: policy exists only on a computer, never communicated　　
4.1.2-②Organisational separation in practiceCompare the organisation chart with appointment documents to confirm the Quality Manager does not also serve as business/marketing manager and has authority to stop activities affecting resultsGood: duties separated; Quality Manager's authority is independent.Red line: Quality Manager also manages sales and is assessed on revenue　　
4.1.3-①Risk identification coverageCheck the impartiality risk register covers four categories: the laboratory's own activities, relationships with parent body/shareholders, personnel relationships (family/second job/re-employment), and external service providersGood: register is classified into four categories with sources stated.Red line: merely writes “no risks” without analysis　　
4.1.3-②Trigger-based identificationSample one new-partner onboarding and one new employee joining; check whether impartiality risk identification was re-triggered at those pointsGood: trigger points and records exist.Red line: never updated; identified only once at the start of the year　　
4.1.4-①Risk measures implementedAgainst the risk register, verify one by one the measures to eliminate or minimise each risk and their implementation evidence (e.g. recusal, disclosure, segregation, monitoring)Good: each risk has a corresponding measure + evidence.Red line: risks identified but no measures　　
4.1.5-①Periodic review recordsCheck the two most recent annual impartiality reviews, confirming that risk changes and measure effectiveness were reviewedGood: reviewed annually with conclusions.Red line: no review, or none in two years　　
4.2.1-①Confidentiality obligations signedSample at least 2 each of customer contract confidentiality clauses, employee confidentiality agreements, and agreements with external personnel (calibration/maintenance) — confirm legal enforceabilityGood: all three categories complete and in force.Red line: external personnel have no confidentiality undertaking　　
4.2.2-①Scope of information definedCheck whether the confidentiality procedure defines the information to be protected; sample one statutory disclosure or customer-consented disclosure for decision and approval recordsGood: scope defined + disclosures approved.Red line: disclosures made at will without records　　
4.2.3-①Notification in placeCheck evidence of how customers are informed (contract annex / training / website statement); interview one customer-facing contact to confirm awareness of confidentiality obligationsGood: contract clause + training as dual channels.Red line: staff do not know the confidentiality boundary　　
4.2.4-①Source anonymisationSample 2 reports/registers covering subcontracted or client-sourced items; check whether the source's identity is anonymised or maskedGood: source information not disclosed to unrelated parties.Red line: report directly shows the upstream customer name of the client　　

§5Structural RequirementsLegal entity · Responsibilities and authority · Management

Checks whether the laboratory is a legally responsible entity, management is clearly defined, responsibility interfaces are unambiguous, and key positions genuinely have authority matched by accountability.
ItemAudit item (what to check)Verification method (how to check)Conformity criteria (good / red line)Evidence recordJudgement
5.1-①Legal entity responsibilityCheck business licence / legal-entity certificate / parent-body authorisation to confirm the laboratory can assume legal liabilityGood: authorisation clear; responsible entity identified.Red line: a department issues reports externally without authorisation　　
5.2-①Management appointmentCheck management appointment documents; compare names/positions with reality; interview to confirm they actually discharge the duties, not merely hold the titleGood: appointment matches actual discharge.Red line: appointed person differs from the person actually in charge　　
5.3-①Scope of activities documentedCheck the scope-of-activities document; sample conformity statements in marketing material and reports for claims beyond scopeGood: scope clear; marketing not exaggerated.Red line: marketing claims parameters outside the accredited scope　　
5.4-①Multi-site consistencyCheck control documents and records for activities outside the fixed facility (on-site/mobile/customer facilities) confirming operation under the same criteriaGood: field work has controlled documents + records.Red line: on-site testing without controlled documents　　
5.5-①Clear responsibility interfacesCheck organisation chart and position descriptions; sample 2–3 interface processes (sample flow / report sign-off) to verify no overlap and no gapsGood: each interface has an accountable person.Red line: samples stall between steps with no owner　　
5.6-①Key position authorityCheck appointment and authorisation letters for quality/technical managers; interview on actual cases of “found a problem → had the authority to stop → did they stop → did they report”Good: real stop-work cases exist and were reported.Red line: authorisation letter without real power; never stopped anything　　
5.7-①Communication of effectivenessCheck communication records (meetings/briefings) confirming management communicates the importance of system effectiveness and meeting customer requirementsGood: regular communication evidence exists.Red line: no communication records　　
5.7-②Changes keep integritySample one recent organisational/process change; check it was planned and implemented while maintaining system integrity (review + transition + training)Good: change reviewed with transition.Red line: abrupt change causing a gap in responsibilities　　

§6Resource RequirementsPersonnel · Facilities · Equipment · Traceability · Supplies

Resource clauses underpin the validity of results. Focus: is competence evidenced, is equipment controlled and traceable, is traceability unbroken.
ItemAudit item (what to check)Verification method (how to check)Conformity criteria (good / red line)Evidence recordJudgement
6.1-①Adequacy of resourcesAgainst workload and accredited scope over the last 12 months, assess whether the quantity and competence of personnel/facilities/equipment/support services matchGood: resource-load comparison exists.Red line: equipment cannot be scheduled at peaks with no countermeasure　　
6.2-①Competence requirements documentedCheck competence requirement documents for positions; sample 3 personnel competence files against position requirements (education/qualification/training/experience)Good: person-position match evidenced.Red line: no competence requirement documents　　
6.2-②Formal authorisationCheck authorisation matrix for key activities: sampling, method operation, result evaluation and sign-off, opinions and interpretations; verify on-station operators match the authorised scopeGood: authorisation is to specific activities + persons.Red line: reports signed without authorisation　　
6.2-③Continuing competence monitoringCheck competence monitoring plan (blind samples/intercomparison/supervision) and records; interview one new employee to verify grasp of operating requirementsGood: regular supervision + records.Red line: no re-assessment after authorisation　　
6.2-④External personnel controlCheck whether seconded/contract personnel are subject to competence confirmation and supervision rather than working directlyGood: external personnel managed equally.Red line: temporary staff operate without confirmation　　
6.3-①Environment controlledCheck continuity and authenticity of temperature/humidity/cleanliness records; check segregation of incompatible activities (e.g. balance room vs vibration source)Good: records continuous + segregation effective.Red line: records clearly backfilled or fabricated　　
6.3-②Out-of-control handlingSample one environmental excursion record; trace whether activities were stopped, prior results assessed and correction takenGood: excursion closed with assessment.Red line: continued producing data when out of limits　　
6.4-①Complete equipment record chainSample 3 key instruments across the full chain: register → calibration certificate → result confirmation → intermediate checks → usage records → status labellingGood: full chain closed and traceable.Red line: missing calibration certificate or confirmation record　　
6.4-②Calibration result confirmationCheck whether the calibration certificate is confirmed by the technical manager on return (range/accuracy meets the method) and the confirmation is signedGood: confirmation signed with conclusions.Red line: certificate filed without confirmation, then equipment used　　
6.4-③Intermediate checksCheck intermediate check plan (objects/method/frequency/criteria) and execution records; sample one instrument overdue for checksGood: plan + execution + criteria.Red line: plan has no criteria, or checks missed　　
6.4-④Defective equipment traceabilityCheck labelling and disposition of defective/withdrawn equipment; assess whether impact on prior results was tracedGood: withdrawal labelling + impact assessment.Red line: faulty equipment not isolated　　
6.5-①Unbroken traceabilitySample 2 parameter traceability chains (equipment → calibration certificate → upstream standard) confirming unbroken chain to SI or a recognised standardGood: chain complete with certificates.Red line: broken chain or expired certificate　　
6.5-②Non-SI traceabilityCheck that parameters that cannot be traced to SI use technical means (reference materials/recognised methods) with evidence retainedGood: alternative evidence exists.Red line: claims traceability but no evidence　　
6.5-③Reference materialsSample 2 batches of reference materials: certificates, validity, acceptance and issue recordsGood: in validity + accepted.Red line: expired reference materials still in use　　
6.6-①Supplier evaluationCheck approved supplier list, selection criteria, evaluation and approval records; sample 2 suppliers' performance monitoring (calibration/consumables)Good: list + periodic evaluation.Red line: used without evaluation　　
6.6-②Subcontracting controlSample one subcontracted project across the full chain: customer consent → subcontractor competence evidence → accountability for resultsGood: consent + competence + clear responsibility.Red line: subcontracted without consent　　

§7Process RequirementsContracts · Methods · Sampling · Records · Reporting · Complaints · Nonconformity

Process clauses directly determine result quality. Focus: is every action supported by objective records, are deviations controlled, and can issued results be traced and recalled when problems emerge.
ItemAudit item (what to check)Verification method (how to check)Conformity criteria (good / red line)Evidence recordJudgement
7.1-①Contract reviewSample 3 contracts/work orders across the full review chain; focus on contracts with decision rules and deviations — reviewed before signingGood: review signed + capability covered.Red line: verbal orders with no records　　
7.1-②Deviations and changesCheck whether deviations from the contract are recorded in writing and the customer notified; whether post-start changes are re-reviewedGood: deviations confirmed with the customer.Red line: changes made unilaterally without notice　　
7.2-①Standards currentCheck standard-currency review records against the method list; sample 2 methods confirming the versions in use are currentGood: review traceable + current.Red line: obsolete standard in use　　
7.2-②Method verification/validationSample 2 methods for verification/validation records (objective/scope/procedure/results/conclusion); check one deviation's approval chainGood: validation complete + deviation authorised.Red line: new method used before verification　　
7.3-①Sampling reproducibleSample 2 sampling records against the sampling plan; check method/date-time/sampler/environment/diagram are completeGood: information complete + reproducible.Red line: sampling without plan or records　　
7.4-①Unique identificationSample 3 items across the full flow from receipt to report; verify the number stays consistent and unbroken; check on-site storage labellingGood: same number across the chain + status clear.Red line: numbers confused or lost　　
7.4-②Anomalies and storageCheck whether abnormal condition at receipt is recorded; whether storage conditions are controlled to avoid mix-up/damage; retained-sample managementGood: anomalies recorded + storage controlled.Red line: anomalies unrecorded, tested anyway　　
7.5-①Records reproducibleSample 5 original records against the “reproducible” standard (observations/data/calculations/person/equipment/environment/date), element by elementGood: enough information to reproduce.Red line: only results recorded, no process　　
7.5-②Amendment disciplineCheck whether amendments are struck through and legible with signature and date; sample one electronic record verifying permissions and amendment trailGood: strike-through + trail.Red line: illegible erasure / overwriting　　
7.6-①Uncertainty evaluationSample 2 parameters' uncertainty evaluation documents; sanity-check against recent PT results; check reference to uncertainty in reports where requiredGood: key components covered + corroborated.Red line: not evaluated where required / contradicts PT　　
7.7-①QC coverageCheck the QC plan and its coverage matrix against the accredited scope; confirm all items and personnel are coveredGood: matrix fully covers.Red line: some items without QC　　
7.7-②Out-of-control and PTSample 3 QC records including one out-of-control handling chain; check that an unsatisfactory PT result was processed as nonconforming workGood: excursion analysed and corrected.Red line: out-of-control not acted on　　
7.8-①Report elementsSample 5 reports element by element against the template (title/lab/customer/method/results/signature/date); check subcontracting is statedGood: elements complete + subcontracting clear.Red line: missing elements / subcontracting not stated　　
7.8-②Opinions and amendmentsCheck that reports with opinions/interpretations are authorised; sample one amended report for full version retention and amendment rulesGood: opinions authorised + versions retained.Red line: opinions added without authorisation / amendments without trail　　
7.9-①Complaint closureSample 1–2 complaint cases end to end (receipt → evaluation → decision → response); check the register and annual analysisGood: closed + fed back into improvement.Red line: complaints unanswered and unregistered　　
7.10-①Nonconforming work managementSample 2 nonconforming work cases end to end; check the four steps “identify → evaluate → dispose → verify” are complete and authorisedGood: four steps complete + impact on issued results assessed.Red line: corrected without impact assessment　　
7.11-①Data systems controlledSample one system's validation/verification and change records; demonstrate permission levels live; check recent backup/restore drillGood: validation + changes + backup.Red line: system used without validation　　

§8Management Requirements (Option A)Documents · Records · Risk · Improvement · Internal audit · Management review

Management clauses focus on the system's self-driving capability. Focus: are documents current, do corrective actions hit root causes, do internal audit and management review genuinely close the loop.
ItemAudit item (what to check)Verification method (how to check)Conformity criteria (good / red line)Evidence recordJudgement
8.2-①Policy and objectives measurableCheck that the policy and objectives are documented and communicated; interview 2 positions on awareness; check objective data sources and statisticsGood: objectives measurable + statistics kept.Red line: slogan-style, non-measurable objectives　　
8.3-①Documents currentSample 3 documents' version chains; randomly check 2 on-site documents in use are current; check standard currency reviewGood: versions clear + in-use documents current.Red line: old revision in use on-site　　
8.3-②Obsolete documents withdrawnCheck whether obsolete documents are withdrawn or retained with a marker to prevent misuseGood: obsolete marked/withdrawn.Red line: old revisions scattered on-site　　
8.4-①Record lifecycleSample 2 record categories against identification/access/storage/backup/archive/retrieval/retention/disposition; check borrowing on-siteGood: retention defined + backups.Red line: no retention period defined　　
8.5-①Risk and opportunity integratedCheck risk register update history; sample 2 measures' effectiveness evaluations; check customer feedback channel and its use in analysisGood: risks integrated + evaluated.Red line: identified but never updated　　
8.6-①Improvement with resultsCheck improvement register; sample 1 improvement case to verify actual results (not merely planned)Good: case exists with results.Red line: plans only, nothing landed　　
8.7-①Corrective action six stepsSample 3 corrective actions against a) reaction, b) root cause, c) action, d) effectiveness, e) risk update, f) system changeGood: root-cause analysis + effectiveness.Red line: re-training only, no root cause　　
8.8-①Internal audit planned and executedCheck year-round execution against LP-02 and IA-01; sample the most recent audit's full file (IA-01–IA-06); verify independence confirmationGood: on plan + independent + closed.Red line: auditors audit their own departments　　
8.9-①Management review inputs completeCheck the most recent management review records (MR series); verify evidence for all 15 inputs one by oneGood: all 15 inputs evidenced.Red line: hollow inputs without data　　
8.9-②Management review outputs implementedCheck the 4 outputs (effectiveness/improvement/resources/changes) each have an owner + deadline and are tracked in MR-06Good: decisions trackable.Red line: report written, nothing tracked　　
Judgement & statistics: after completing this form, register all × nonconformities by item and clause into IA-05 (Nonconformity Distribution & Trend Analysis) to identify system weak points; list ○ observations separately in the audit report (FR-25). File this form and its evidence column under record control per §8.4, retention no less than 6 years.
