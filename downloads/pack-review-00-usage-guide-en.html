5　Usage Advice for Purchasers

① First build understanding with MR-00 → LP-09 → MR-WI-01; ② replace the grey placeholder text in MR-01 through MR-07 with your laboratory's real information; ③ on first use, run through all nine steps once, then reuse and refine annually; ④ all forms can be printed as controlled records or migrated into your own document system; ⑤ pair with the internal audit pack (IA series): FR-25 is the key evidence for management review input e).
