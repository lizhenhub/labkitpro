NVLAP · Specific Requirements Beyond ISO/IEC 17025:2017

NVLAP · Specific Requirements Relative to ISO/IEC 17025:2017 — Explained

NVLAP (National Voluntary Laboratory Accreditation Program) is operated by the US National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST). Its most distinctive feature: ISO/IEC 17025:2017 is implemented through the combination of the NIST Handbook 150 general handbook, the 150-x program-specific handbooks, and Lab Bulletin (LB) inserts — general requirements live in the handbook, program requirements in 150-x, and every latest revision is issued as an LB. Reading the handbook but not the bulletins inevitably means missing requirements.

!Key Points

The core combination laboratories must implement first is NIST Handbook 150 (general procedures) + the HB 150-x handbook of their program + the matching Lab Bulletin (LB) inserts + the General Criteria Checklist; handbooks are refreshed only every few years — reading handbooks without LBs inevitably misses current requirements.

1

Relationship to ISO/IEC 17025:2017

Handbooks + bulletins combined

NVLAP implements ISO/IEC 17025:2017 under the ILAC MRA framework, but splits requirements into a handbook layer plus a bulletin layer: general procedures in NIST Handbook 150, program requirements in the 150-x series, and all revisions and additions issued as Lab Bulletin inserts, with checklists delivered through the NIWS portal.

Layered document system

Layer 1 · International baselineISO/IEC 17025:2017 (transposed by NVLAP under the ILAC MRA; LB-116/117-2020 updated accreditation documents uniformly)
Layer 2 · General handbookNIST Handbook 150, NVLAP Procedures and General Requirements (2020 ed., Update 1) — common to all programs
Layer 3 · Program-specific handbooksNIST HB 150-x series (20 programs: calibration / asbestos / EMC / cryptographic security / construction materials / radiation dosimetry, etc.)
Layer 4 · Lab Bulletin insertsLB series (40+ current items from 2020–2026) — revisions, clarifications and new requirements to the handbooks, currently in force
Layer 5 · Checklists & portalGeneral Criteria Checklist + program-specific checklists (obtained via the NIWS lab portal) + Policy Guidance PG series

Practical Points & Common Pitfalls

6 tips

NVLAP runs on the dual track of "handbooks + bulletins"; the bulletin layer is exactly what is most easily missed.

1Handbook + bulletin combo: missing bulletins means missing requirements

General requirements are in HB 150, program requirements in HB 150-x, and current revisions are all in LB inserts — handbooks are refreshed only every few years; the LB is the current requirement.NVLAP's unique "vehicle of current requirements" mechanism

2Checklists come from the NIWS portal, not public downloads

Since 2024, program checklists moved to the NIWS Lab Portal (LB-156); the General Criteria Checklist must be obtained by contacting NVLAP — register a portal account in advance.Not having the checklist before applying is a common bottleneck

3CST pauses new applications in 2026 — confirm eligibility first

LB-163-2026 pauses new CST applications and LB-160-2025 terminated SCAP accreditation — labs in cryptographic security should verify the status before planning.Program-level pauses directly affect admission

4ECT: pick the checklist by sub-program

150-11A (FCC) / 11B (ISED Canada) / 11C (FDA ASCA) correspond to different accreditation targets — choose the right checklist for your business.ISED recognition additionally requires the LB-159 application documents

5Check LB-166-2026 first for asbestos renewals

The PT documentation requirements have changed for asbestos (PLM/TEM) renewal applications — prepare proficiency-testing records per the new requirements before renewing.Incomplete renewal materials get rejected

6Revision nodes concentrate in 2024–2026 — verify before citing

HB 150-2/3/18 have all been revised (LB-158/161/154) and the General Criteria Checklist was updated 2026-09-10 — citing an old edition for an assessment is a common error during revision periods.Versions follow the NVLAP website Lab Bulletin list
