4　Triple-Dimension Judgement (writing model)

[Template] Based on the evidence of the 15 inputs: ① Suitability — the system still matches the current workload (+18%), extended scope and regulatory environment (new metrology law in force); no structural adjustment needed; ② Adequacy — elements complete; 4 procedure revisions during the year cover all changes; no gaps; ③ Effectiveness — quality objective attainment rate 83% (5/6), PT satisfaction rate 100%, report error rate 0.4%; the system achieves the policy and objectives and ensures result validity. Overall judgement: the management system is continually suitable, adequate and effective.

Output Wording: Good / Poor Comparison

Poor: “a system effective; b continuous improvement; c suggest adding equipment; d revise documents in due course.” — no owner, no deadline, no verifiable criteria.

Good: “c Resources: the Technical Manager to complete procurement, installation and metrological traceability of a gas chromatograph by 2027-03-31 (acceptance: equipment register + traceability certificates); d Changes: the Quality Manager to adjust the report on-time target from 95% to 93% by 2026-12-31 and revise the 2027 quality objectives (acceptance: release of the new objectives document).”
