CNAS laboratory accreditation · industry-classified document list

CNAS Accreditation Documents by Industry (Chinese mainland)

Beyond the general criteria, this list re-groups all industry-specific documents by testing field / industry (A-series field application documents + specific-type laboratory criteria RL / CL + related GL / TRL / EL / S documents), covering manufacturing, energy, construction, building materials, healthcare, forensics, quarantine, food, environment, IT, new energy, biology and every other industry suited to CNAS accreditation — so each laboratory can quickly lock onto the documents it needs. Document titles without an official English edition are shown in Chinese.

Note: the general criteria (R-series rules, RL01–RL04 laboratory rules, CNAS-CL01 core criteria, G-series general criteria) are the essential foundation for all testing / calibration labs and are not repeated here. The documents below are grouped by industry / testing field around the field application documents (A series) and their companion documents, and include the specific rules (RL) and criteria (CL) with their application documents for specific body types (medical, forensics, quarantine, PTP, RMP, research, biobanks). Documents marked 2026 / 2025 new or latest are recent CNAS issues or re-issues — always verify the current edition on the CNAS website.

Scope

This list contains only industry-specific documents outside the general criteria (R / RL01–RL04 / CL01 / G series), grouped by testing field / industry. Grouping is anchored on the CNAS-CL01-A series field application documents, and also includes specific rules (RL05–RL10) and criteria (CL02–CL10) for specific laboratory / body types (medical, forensics, quarantine, PTP, RMP, research, biobanks) with their application documents, plus related GL / TRL / EL / S documents. Version information follows live CNAS releases.

How to use

1) First locate your industry group by the testing / calibration field or body type you apply for; 2) in each group the A-series application documents (or specific criteria / rules) are the essential core, with GL / TRL / EL as guidance and competence statements; 3) for documents marked 2026 / 2025 latest or new, always verify the current edition on the CNAS website.
