1

ILAC

International Laboratory Accreditation CooperationInternational mutual-recognition layer

ILAC is not an accreditation body — it is the global framework for mutual recognition of laboratory accreditation (ILAC MRA). Labs do not apply to ILAC; they are accredited by their national body, which holds ILAC MRA signatory status. On 2026-01-01 ILAC ceased operating and its functions moved to Global ACI (global-aci.org); document numbering is transitioning. The policies below bind labs through their national body.

1General obligations

  • P8 Use of symbol & claims of accreditation status — mandatory before claiming ILAC MRA on export reportsKept effective by Global ACI 2025-29 decision; mapping to MRA-006
  • R7 Rules for the ILAC MRA markPairs with P8; governs the combined mark
  • P9 Proficiency testing policyThe umbrella framework for PT participation; → TECH-1-008

2Core criteria

  • No standard of its own — base standards such as ISO/IEC 17025:2017 (testing/calibration) and ISO 15189:2022 (medical) are converted and enforced by each national ABILAC's A-series peer evaluation keeps AB enforcement consistent

3Sector requirements

  • P15 Application of ISO/IEC 17020:2012 (inspection) → TECH-2-001
  • G19 Forensic science · G26 Medical accreditation · G7/G29 Horse racing & anti-dopingOnly if your field applies
  • G27/G28 Measurement in inspection, scope wording for inspection bodies

4Metrology · uncertainty · PT

  • P10 Metrological traceability policy→ TECH-1-011 in draft; remains effective meanwhile
  • P14 MU in calibration · G17 MU in testingCalibration vs testing labs respectively
  • G8 Decision rules & statements of conformity · G18 Scope description · G24 Recalibration intervalsReporting judgments, scope filing, cal-cycle management

5Application & assessment

  • No direct application channel — MRA eligibility runs through your national body's IAF/ILAC A2 peer evaluationA2 decides your AB's signatory status; 2025-09 revision maps to MRA-005

6Maintenance & recent changes

  • ILAC→Global ACI transition: P9→TECH-1-008, P14→TECH-1-009, P10→TECH-1-011 (draft)Watch the new numbers adopted by your AB
  • Archive at ilac.org for reference; live documents at global-aci.org
Key point: At the ILAC layer only three things matter for a lab — traceability (P10), proficiency testing (P9), and symbol & claims (P8/R7). Confirm the current numbering your AB forwards before putting the MRA mark on export reports.

CNAS

China National Accreditation Service for Conformity AssessmentMainland China

The only accreditation body for mainland China, with the most complete file system: general rules (R/RL) + basic criteria (CL) + sector application notes (A) + guides (GL) + explanatory notes (EL) + technical reports (TRL). Step 1 is locking your sector; step 2 is picking the matching A-series.

1General obligations

  • RL01:2025 Accreditation rules · RL02:2023 Proficiency testing rulesAnnual PT per sector; non-compliance affects accreditation
  • R01 Symbol use & claims · R02 Impartiality & confidentiality · R03:2026 Appeals / complaints / disputesR03 revised 2026-09-22, replacing the 2019 edition
  • RL03:2025 Fees · RL04 Acceptance of overseas labs

2Core criteria

  • CL01:2018 Accreditation criteria (identical to ISO/IEC 17025:2017)
  • CL01-G001:2024 Application requirements (mandatory) · G002 Traceability · G003 Uncertainty · G004 Internal calibrationG001: 2nd revision with substantive changes in 2024
  • G005 Non-fixed-site testing activitiesApplies when field/temporary-site testing exists

3Sector requirements

  • CL01-A series Application notes (40 industry groups; pick by sector)High-frequency: A001 microbiology, A002 chemistry, A025 calibration, A008 EMC
  • Recent revisions: A004 medical devices (eff. 2026-05-01), A018 construction (eff. 2027-01-01), A016 sensory (2025-01)
  • Special programs S01–S06 (Energy Star, Bluetooth, anti-doping, EPA, OIML…) by business

4Metrology · uncertainty · PT

  • RL02 Annual PT requirements · GL002/GL032 PT result statistics & selection review
  • GL006 Chemistry uncertainty · GL025:2023 CMC expression · GL042 Intermediate checks · GL054:2023 Calibration intervalsCalibration labs focus

5Application & assessment

  • EL-15 Acceptance requirements · EL-03:2023 Scope wording · EL-13 Reports/certificates · EL-20 Remote assessment · EL-28 English name/address filing
  • GL001 Accreditation guide · GL008 NC grading · GL011/GL012:2025 Internal audit & management review guides (2025-12)

6Maintenance & recent changes

  • Surveillance per RL01; PT per RL02
  • 2026 additions/revisions: R03:2026, biobank RL10:2026 + EL-30:2026, coal TRL-026/027, advanced manufacturing TRL-024/025, lab animals CL06-A001:2026Technical-report list updated 2026-08-28
Key point: Lock your CL01-A series (40 groups) first, then complete the G-series quartet (G001–G004). Schedule PT early per RL02 — it is the most common pre-assessment failure.

A2LA

American Association for Laboratory AccreditationUSA (non-governmental)

US non-profit, non-governmental accreditation body (ISO/IEC 17011; ILAC/APAC recognition). Files fall into six families: A organization / I information / P policy / R requirements (mandatory) / G guides / F & A308 forms. The R-series are hard requirements; the P-series are policies.

1General obligations

  • R101 General accreditation requirements for CABs (2026-09-01)Foundation for all CAB accreditation
  • R102 Conditions of accreditation (2026-02-20) · R105 Referencing A2LA accreditation statusHard constraints when publicly claiming accreditation
  • R103 PT requirements for ISO/IEC 17025 labs (2026-04-01)

2Core criteria

  • ISO/IEC 17025:2017 implemented via R101
  • Medical labs: R602 conditions + R603 PT requirements (ISO 15189 + CLIA)

3Sector requirements

  • R208 Construction materials · R212 NDT · R221 Forensic testing (MD/TX appendices) · R255 Electrical · R262 State legal metrology · R310 Special inspection bodies · R311 FedRAMP
  • P103a–e Uncertainty annexes by sector: automotive / life sciences / EMC / construction & geotech / forensicsPick the annex matching your testing field

4Metrology · uncertainty · PT

  • P102 Traceability policy (2024-10-31) + G135/G149 guidesIncludes internal calibration
  • P103 Testing MU policy (2025-08-01) · G104 Testing MU guide (2026-08-26)
  • G133 PT program guide · G136 Decision rules in calibration

5Application & assessment

  • F-series Scope selection lists (by field: F204 cal / F205 chemistry / F209 electrical…)
  • F117 Technical personnel matrix · F349 Subcontractor capability form · I105 Typical application stepsF210a/F229 need client/partner portal login

6Maintenance & recent changes

  • 2026 dense revisions: P120 satellite labs (2026-08), P121 anti-fraud (2026-08), P105 relocation (2026-05), P112 flexible scope (2026-03), G118 cal-scope definition (2026-10), G126/G149 uncertainty guidesAlways verify version dates before applying
  • Medical R602/R603 updated 2025-10
Key point: A2LA revises documents densely in 2026. Base your file set on R101 + your sector R2xx + P102/P103; file scope with the F-series lists; F117 is the official carrier of personnel qualification.

UKAS

United Kingdom Accreditation ServiceUnited Kingdom

The UK national accreditation body (ISO/IEC 17011). UKAS labs must comply with three layers simultaneously: UKAS's own documents (GEN/LAB/TPS) + mandatory EA policies + Global ACI (formerly ILAC) policies. This stacked requirement is UKAS's signature.

1General obligations

  • Terms for use of the UK national accreditation symbol (official gov.uk document)
  • GEN 1 General assessment principles (2025-03, Ed.4) · GEN 4 Flexible scope policy (2024-11) · GEN 6 Referencing accreditation status & MRA signatoriesGEN 4 replaces LAB 39/TPS 59; GEN 6 replaces LAB 1

2Core criteria

  • ISO/IEC 17025:2017 / ISO 15189:2022; ISO 15195:2018 for reference-measurement labs
  • LAB 5 Calibration result reporting · LAB 12 Testing uncertainty (2025-12) · LAB 13 Opinions & interpretations (2026-09) · LAB 45 Calibration scope schedule · LAB 48 Decision rules · M3003 Uncertainty & confidence

3Sector requirements

  • LAB 30 Asbestos · LAB 33 Official food/feed & national reference labs (2025-12) · LAB 37 Drinking water DWTS · LAB 32 National DNA database · LAB 201 On-site forensics
  • 2025 additions: LAB 60 genomic medicine, LAB 61 networked / multi-site medical servicesMedical labs focus
  • LAB 11/14/15/25 Temperature, weights, volumetric glassware, hardness metrology guides

4Metrology · uncertainty · PT

  • TPS 41 Traceability policy · TPS 47 PT participation (Ed.6 under review) · TPS 57 Reference material selection
  • Mandatory Global ACI: TECH-1-008 (PT, replaces ILAC P9) · TECH-1-009 (calibration MU, replaces P14) · ILAC P10 (traceability)

5Application & assessment

  • TPS 53 Report/certificate claim wording · TPS 63 Out-of-specification samples · TPS 76 Temporary facilities (new 2024-11)
  • Mandatory EA: EA-4/02 calibration MU · EA-3/01 marks & MLA reference · EA-4/17 medical scope · EA-2/15 flexible scope

6Maintenance & recent changes

  • Track three layers in parallel: UKAS + EA + Global ACI revisions
  • TPS 71 Medical diagnostic pathways (2026-05, Ed.3) · LAB 24 Ed.3 in consultation · TPS 47 Ed.6 under review
Key point: UKAS is about stacking — besides UKAS's own documents, EA and Global ACI policies are mandatory. Track three publication lists (UKAS M4 publications + EA library + Global ACI library).

NVLAP

National Voluntary Laboratory Accreditation Program · NISTUSA (government program)

A US government program run by NIST under 15 CFR Part 285; criteria aligned to ISO/IEC 17025:2017. Unlike A2LA, NVLAP is a statutorily authorized government program organized by LAP (Laboratory Accreditation Program). Current requirements = project handbook + inserted Lab Bulletins.

1General obligations

  • NIST HB 150 Procedures & general requirements (2020 edition, Update 1 — applies to all programs)
  • Must insert two current bulletins: LB-156-2024 (program checklists now via NIWS portal) · LB-132-2020 (NIWS root-cause analysis for NCs)

2Core criteria

  • ISO/IEC 17025:2017 aligned; transition bulletins LB-116/117-2020 must be inserted
  • PG-17-2024 Policy guide: accreditation expiry = voluntary terminationMatters for renewal management

3Sector requirements

  • HB 150-x Project handbooks (20 current), pick by LAP: 150-2 calibration (2024), 150-3 bulk asbestos (2025), 150-5 construction materials, 150-8 acoustics, 150-11 EMC/telecom (FCC/ISED/ASCA), 150-17 crypto security, 150-18 fastener metal (2024), 150-31 health IT
  • Handbook + its Lab Bulletins = current requirements for that program

4Metrology · uncertainty · PT

  • PT per handbook & bulletins: LB-166-2026 asbestos renewal must submit PT recordsKey change for renewal applications
  • Other LAPs set their own PT frequency and records (see handbook)

5Application & assessment

  • On-site checklists (26) distributed via the NIWS portal since 2024 — not public PDFsGeneral criteria checklist (rev. 2026-09-10) available by request from NVLAP
  • Checklists with * work together with a Test Method Review Summary

6Maintenance & recent changes

  • LB-163-2026 crypto (CST) pauses new applications · LB-164-2026 OWM collaboration (calibration) · LB-165-2026 HIT health data rules
  • Numbering gaps (150-7/9/12…) are terminated programs, no longer listed
Key point: NVLAP's classic trap is reading the handbook without the bulletins. Handbook + bulletins = current requirements; checklists come only through the NIWS portal — request your authorized account early.

NATA

National Association of Testing Authorities, AustraliaAustralia

Australia's government-endorsed national body with the broadest coverage: testing / calibration / inspection / medical / GLP / PTP / RMP / biobanking / imaging / sleep / respiratory. Its signature is the Standards Application Document (SAD) — Australia-specific requirements added on top of the international standards.

1General obligations

  • NATA Rules Accreditation rules (2026-09-11 + Amendment Sheet)
  • NATA Procedures (2026-08-25) · Accreditation Service Commitment (2025-10)
  • GAC Use of the NATA mark, endorsements & claims (2025-06)

2Core criteria

  • SAD 17025 Application of ISO/IEC 17025:2017 (testing/calibration core; NATA signature)
  • SAD 15189 Medical testing (eff. 2026-02) · SAD 17020:2026 (eff. 2026-10) · SAD 17043:2023 · SAD 17034

3Sector requirements

  • SAC Appendices (47): calibration (8), life sciences, infrastructure & asset integrity (IAI), law-forensic, manufactured goods, materials
  • SoA Schedule of Accreditation scope (basis for scope submission — one per industry)
  • GAC Multi-site organizations (2024-12) · New branch sites (2026-09)

4Metrology · uncertainty · PT

  • GAC Metrological traceability (2020-12) · Equipment assurance, internal calibration & verification (2019-05)
  • Policy PT participation (2021-12) · Remote assessment (2022-12)

5Application & assessment

  • Worksheet ISO/IEC 17025 assessment worksheet (2025-09) · ISO 15189 worksheet (for labs)
  • FDU Facilities detail update · Scope change application · Notice of new on-site facility (2026-06) · NDT site consultation form (2026-10)

6Maintenance & recent changes

  • Special schemes: OECD GLP, imaging (RANZCR), sleep (ASA), respiratory (TSANZ)
  • 2026 dense revisions: Rules / Procedures, SAD 17020:2026, SAD 15189 (eff. 2026-02); superseded documents merged into new criteria — do not use old editions
Key point: Read the SAD first — the Australia-specific layer sits on top of the international standard. Submit scope using the SoA schedule for your industry.

SINGLAS

Singapore Laboratory Accreditation Scheme · SACSingapore

Singapore's laboratory accreditation scheme run by the Singapore Accreditation Council (SAC). Covers testing/calibration (ISO/IEC 17025:2017) and medical (ISO 15189:2022). Medical is finely divided — MED 002 issues specific requirements per 8 sub-disciplines, a Singapore signature.

1General obligations

  • SAC 01 General terms & conditions (2026-01-01) · SAC 02 Marks & MRA marks (2026-01-01)
  • PROF 001 PT policy (2026-04-16) · SAC-SINGLAS 004 Test item classification (2026-03-09) · SAC-SINGLAS 003 Fee schedule (eff. 2024-12-01)

2Core criteria

  • SAC-SINGLAS 001 Accreditation procedure (17025 ver. 2026-03-09; medical 2024-09-06)
  • SAC-SINGLAS 006 Measurement traceability (2021-05) · LAFM 03 ISO 17025 assessment checklist (2026-03-27) · LAFM 03-MED ISO 15189 checklist

3Sector requirements

  • MET 001/002 Calibration · C&B and ENV 001/002 Chemical/biological/environmental (shared) · CE 001/002 Civil (incl. concrete NDT) · EL 001/002 Electrical · FFT 01 Functional food · MECH 001 Mechanical · NDT 001-003 NDT
  • IT 001 IT security testing (2026-08-08, replaces 2018 edition) · IT 002 Gaming

4Metrology · uncertainty · PT

  • Technical Guide 1 Uncertainty (2026-03-18) · TG 2 Chemistry/microbiology · TG 3 Civil/mechanical · TG 4 Medical (2025-11-03)
  • MET 002 CMC recording criteria · PROF 001 PT requirements

5Application & assessment

  • LAFM 03 / LAFM 03-MED assessment checklists (17025 / 15189)
  • Match your field to its Specific Requirements file (MET / C&B / CE / EL / NDT…)

6Maintenance & recent changes

  • Medical subdivision: MED 001 general (2025-06-30) + MED 002 series — 8 sub-disciplines (clinical chemistry, cytogenetics, cytopathology, haematology, histopathology, genetics & genomics, microbiology, molecular pathology) unified revision 2025-11-03
  • ~20 files revised/issued in 2025–2026 — verify versions before applying
Key point: Medical labs pick the MED 002 for their sub-discipline; non-medical labs follow the field-specific requirements. Version checking is the maintenance priority — SAC 01/02, PROF 001 and IT 001 all changed in 2026.

NABL

National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration LaboratoriesIndia

India's national accreditation board, covering testing/calibration (17025), medical (15189), PTP (17043), RMP (17034) and biobanking (20387). 77 current documents: pick the criteria + application form + assessment checklist triplet by organization type.

1General obligations

  • NABL 100B Accreditation procedure (2025-08-27) · NABL 131 Terms & conditions (2026-01-23)
  • NABL 133 Marks + ILAC MRA joint mark (2024-09) · NABL 134 Appeals (2025-09-30) · NABL 132/132A Complaints

2Core criteria

  • Testing/calibration: ISO/IEC 17025:2017; medical: NABL 112A specific criteria (2024-12-18, per ISO 15189:2022)
  • PTP: NABL 181A (per 17043:2023) · RMP: NABL 191 (per 17034:2016) · Biobank: NABL 229 (per 20387)

3Sector requirements

  • NABL 129 Calibration (mechanical/flow/radiation/electrical/thermal) · NABL 130 On-site testing/calibration · NABL 135 Medical imaging (2026-01) · NABL 137 Software/IT (2026-04) · NABL 224 Temporary sites · NABL 139 State-level drinking water · NABL 226 Government soil · NABL 127 Joint assessment (2026-08)

4Metrology · uncertainty · PT

  • NABL 141 Uncertainty · NABL 142 Traceability · NABL 143 CMC policy
  • NABL 163 PT participation (2024-09) · NABL 164 Inter-lab comparison when no formal PT

5Application & assessment

  • Triplet by type: application form (151 testing / 152 calibration / 153 medical / 180 PTP / 190 RMP / 227 biobank; consolidated revision 2026-08-18) + assessment checklist (NABL 219 per 17025:2017; 223 per 15189:2022) + document review checklist (NABL 220 series by standard, 220/220C-F)
  • NABL 209/208A Pre-assessment · NABL 210 Assessor guide

6Maintenance & recent changes

  • Annual surveillance NABL 218A + desktop surveillance NABL 218 (rev. 2026-01)
  • 2026 additions: NABL 233/234 defence aviation DGAQA (2026-08-11), NABL 237/238 NACO network accreditation (2026-10-15)For labs in those sectors
Key point: NABL runs on triplet logic — criteria (112A/129/181A/191/229) + application form + assessment/document-review checklists. Desktop surveillance and the annual surveillance checklist are your two maintenance handles.
