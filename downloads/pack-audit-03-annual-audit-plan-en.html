5　Reporting and Record Requirements

Each audit is executed per LP-02: report issued within 10 working days of the audit (FR-25); correction evidence submitted within 15 working days of the nonconformity; year-end closure consolidated by 30 November (IA-05, IA-06) as management review input (§8.9.2 e)).
