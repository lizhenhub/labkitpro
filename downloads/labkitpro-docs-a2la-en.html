A2LA Laboratory Accreditation Document List

A2LA (American Association for Laboratory Accreditation) is a non-profit, non-governmental third-party accreditation body recognised to ISO/IEC 17011 and a signatory to the ILAC/APAC mutual recognition arrangements. This list covers every public document currently offered on the A2LA documents portal (portal.a2la.org/documents), organised into six categories — organisational (A), information (I), policy (P), accreditation requirements (R), guidance (G) and forms (F/A308) — with official numbers, original English titles, publication dates and availability.

How to use

R-series documents are mandatory accreditation requirements, P-series are general policies, G-series are technical guidance, and F/A308 are application forms. F210a and F229 are currently flagged as permission-restricted on the portal (client/partner login required); F337 and F338 are online Microsoft Forms. The current edition on the A2LA website always governs.
