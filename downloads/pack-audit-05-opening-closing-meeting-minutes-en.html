Opening & Closing Meeting Record

Two meetings, one form: the front page records the opening meeting agenda and attendance; the back page records the closing meeting communication and attendance. Meeting records are direct evidence of the “documented information” requirement of the audit.
Form No. IA-03 Version V1.0 Supports LP-02 §5.4.1 / §5.6

Opening Meeting Agenda Checklist (tick each item)

  • Introduce the audit team members and their allocation
  • Confirm the audit objective (routine / special / pre-scope-extension / pre-assessment)
  • Confirm the audit scope (clauses, departments and locations, activity period) — consistent with IA-02
  • Confirm the audit criteria (standard and system document revisions)
  • Communicate the audit schedule and sampling plan
  • Explain audit methods: document review, record review, on-site observation, interviews; state that the audit is a sampling activity
  • Explain how nonconformities are raised and facts confirmed, and the severity grading
  • State correction requirements and deadlines (correction evidence due within 15 working days)
  • Confirm closing meeting time and venue
  • Explain how objections and appeals are handled (written appeal to the Quality Manager)
  • Confirm escort and liaison arrangements
  • Auditee opening remarks

Closing Meeting Agenda Checklist

  • Thank the audited departments for their cooperation
  • Summarise the audit as a whole (clauses covered, number of items sampled)
  • Read out the findings item by item: major nonconformities ___ , minor nonconformities ___ , observations ___ , opportunities for improvement ___
  • Read out each nonconformity report (FR-24) number, clause and fact description; confirm facts with the auditee
  • Communicate the observation and improvement-opportunity lists
  • Announce the audit conclusion (preliminary view on conformity and effectiveness of the system; final position per the report)
  • State correction requirements and deadlines (correction evidence due within 15 working days; for major nonconformities, also assess the impact on issued reports)
  • State report issue timing (within 10 working days) and the appeal channel
  • Auditee closing remarks
