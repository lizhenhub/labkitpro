UKAS (United Kingdom Accreditation Service) Laboratory Accreditation Document List

UKAS (United Kingdom Accreditation Service) is the UK's national accreditation body, accrediting testing / calibration laboratories (ISO/IEC 17025:2017), medical laboratories (ISO 15189:2022) and others under ISO/IEC 17011. Based on the UKAS Publications List (M4, 2026-10-06 edition) and the website section Resources → Publications → Laboratory accreditation, this volume covers all current UKAS publications (GEN / LAB / TPS / IMA series) plus the EA and Global ACI (formerly ILAC/IAF, merged from 2026) documents laboratories must mandatorily adopt, each with edition / date and official PDF links.
