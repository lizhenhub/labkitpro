Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026 · Last updated: October 31, 2026
TL;DR. This extension reads only the YouTube video ID of the page you are watching. It does not read page content, your watch history, open tabs, or any other browsing data. Your video's transcript is fetched on our server and sent to an AI model to generate a summary; the transcript is processed in memory and is never stored. We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

3. How we use your data

  • Device ID + daily counter: to enforce the free plan limit (3 summaries/day) and unlock Pro features.
  • Video ID + transcript + style: to generate your requested summary for the video you are watching.
  • Email (if provided): to associate your Pro subscription with your device and send receipts.
  • Referral data (if you participate): to operate the invite program — bind referrals, prevent duplicate/fraudulent rewards, and grant bonus Pro time to the referrer.

4. Server-side processing & third parties

When you request a summary, the following happens on our infrastructure:

  1. Your browser reads the public captions (transcript) of the YouTube video you are watching and sends the transcript text, the Video ID, and your chosen style to our API at api.labkitpro.com. If the captions cannot be read in your browser, our server attempts to fetch the same public transcript from YouTube's servers instead.
  2. The transcript text is sent to Cloudflare Workers AI (Meta Llama 3.1 Instruct) to generate the summary. DeepSeek is configured as a backup AI provider for failover only.
  3. The summary is returned to your browser. The transcript is processed in memory and is not persisted by us.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers. We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs in the Extension.

5. Data storage & retention

  • Local (your browser): Device ID, auth token, and optional email are stored in chrome.storage.local. These are cleared automatically when you uninstall the Extension.
  • Server (Cloudflare KV): your Device ID, daily quota counter (reset every 24h), and subscription status. If you provided an email, it is stored to link your Pro plan.
  • Transcript: fetched and sent to the AI model on demand; never written to disk or any database by us.
  • Payment data: never stored by us — handled by the payment processor.

6. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are in the EU/EEA (GDPR) or California (CCPA), you have the right to access, correct, and delete your personal data, and to object to its processing. To exercise these rights:

  • Local data: uninstall the Extension — Chrome removes all locally stored data automatically.
  • Server data: email support@labkitpro.com with your Device ID (or the email used for Pro). We will delete the associated record within 30 days. A self-service deletion option will also be added in-product.
  • Opt out of sale/sharing: we do not sell or share your data, so no opt-out is required.

7. Children

The Extension is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction) and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Security

API communication is encrypted over HTTPS. Authentication uses short-lived signed tokens. Secrets (API keys, signing keys) are stored as server-side environment secrets and are never included in the Extension package. We follow least-privilege access on our infrastructure.
