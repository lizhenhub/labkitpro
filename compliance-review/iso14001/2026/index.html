ISO 14001:2026 transition · Compliance Review notes

ISO 14001:2026 has the earliest cut-off of the three systems at 30 April 2029. This page returns sub-clause-level review comments and recommendations on your uploaded EMS documents to align documentation and evidence before the transition audit; it is not an internal audit checklist and produces no audit report. The self-check covers whether the environmental context addresses climate change, biodiversity or ecosystem health, pollution levels and availability of natural resources; whether potential emergency situations are determined under 6.1.2; proportionality of response and widened training under 8.2; outsourced-process control under 8.1; whether life-cycle thinking runs through aspects and operational control; the new 6.3 change planning; and whether compliance-obligation wording and clause 10 references have been updated throughout.

What the 2026 review comments look for

The recurring EMS problem is that an assessment was done once but never updated after change: the aspects register is not revisited after a new line, a new supplier or a legal change; the compliance-obligations list still uses the old wording and omits outsourced activities; the emergency-scenario register does not match the drill records; and life-cycle thinking appears only in the manual, never in supplier evaluation or outsourced-process control. Each review comment names the sub-clause and the record type needed, so remediation can be assigned to the responsible function.

Sequencing against the earliest cut-off

Working back from 2029-04-30: T-18 (around 2027-10) for the gap analysis and the start of document revision; T-9 (around 2028-07) for emergency drills, internal audit and management review; T-3 (around 2029-01) to book the transition audit and confirm the CB has passed its AB transition decision. Because CB transition declarations are due 2027-01-31, confirm your CB arrangements during the first self-review rather than discovering near the deadline that the body is not transitioning.

Where this tool stops

Compliance Review produces findings and recommendations for your own preparation. This is a preparatory self-check for your own transition work. It produces review comments and recommendations, not an audit report, not a certification or accreditation decision, and it does not replace the transition audit performed by your certification body. Note that a separate ISO/IEC 17025:2017 internal audit checklist (FR-25) exists elsewhere on this site for laboratory accreditation — Compliance Review on this page is not an audit checklist. The scope of the transition audit, whether it may be combined with surveillance or recertification, remote auditing and any justification for extra time are decided by the certification body under Global ACI-TECH-3-TR and the related ISO/IEC 17021-1 documents.

Frequently asked questions

What is the biggest difference from a 2015 EMS self-review?

Six new or tightened areas: a widened environmental context with a stronger life-cycle perspective; determining potential emergency situations under 6.1.2; proportionality of response and widened training under 8.2; outsourced-process control under 8.1; new change planning under 6.3; and compliance obligations shifting from fulfil to meet — plus the reference updates caused by the clause 10 reorder.

Can we hand the review comments to our certification body?

Not as audit evidence. It is an internal remediation worksheet; what you should be able to show are the records produced by the remediation — the updated aspects register, drill evaluations, supplier controls and change-review records.

What evidence is needed for outsourced processes?

At minimum: identification of outsourced activities with an environmental-impact judgment, supplier selection and evaluation criteria, environmental requirements in contracts or agreements, and monitoring and performance records. Waste disposal, logistics and outsourced processing are frequently sampled, and the life-cycle perspective brings them into the evaluation scope.

What if our certification body does not transition?

Under the official timeline a CB that decides not to transition must notify affected clients in writing by 2027-01-31, including certificate validity and consequences. Start evaluating alternative bodies as soon as you receive such a notice: a new CB has to schedule a fresh transition audit, and slots tighten as 2029-04-30 approaches.
