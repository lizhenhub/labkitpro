2026-edition transition packs

Turn the new editions into a transition plan you can actually run

ISO 9001:2026 was published on 16 September 2026 and ISO 14001:2026 on 15 April 2026. Neither is a rebuild — both strengthen leadership, quality/environmental culture, a clearer split between risk and opportunity, lifecycle perspective and change planning. Each pack below gives you: a clause-by-clause diff, a gap checklist, a controlled-document revision list, the points auditors will probe, and a milestone timeline back-planned from the cutoff.

ISO 9001:2026 Transition Pack

$19

1 · What actually changed (ISO-confirmed)

  • 5.1 领导作用 — Strengthened: explicit quality culture and ethical behaviour; leaders must create an environment where everyone actively contributes to QMS effectiveness.
  • 5.2 质量方针 — More explicitly linked to context (4.1) and strategic direction — the QMS becomes a tool for business objectives, not just compliance.
  • 6.1 风险与机遇 — Clearer separation of addressing risks vs pursuing opportunities — each used for informed decision-making and continual improvement, not blurred together.
  • 7.3 意识 — New requirement: people must understand quality culture and ethical behaviour, not just know the procedures.
  • 新增 Annex A — Replaces the old informative annex; clarifies key concepts, terminology and clause intent so the standard is easier to apply consistently.
  • 气候变化 — The 2024 amendment’s climate-change consideration is carried into the main text (4.1/4.2).
  • 协调结构 — Adopts the latest harmonized structure for ISO management-system standards, making integration with 14001/45001 easier.

三、差距自查表（逐项勾销）

  • Re-read 5.1: add explicit quality-culture and ethical-behaviour expectations and evidence into your leadership process/roles.
  • Check the quality policy (5.2) explicitly aligns with strategic direction; rewrite the intro if it does not.
  • Split your 6.1 risk register and opportunity register — are they currently blended into one?
  • Update 7.3 awareness material to include quality culture and ethical behaviour, and keep training evidence.
  • Verify 4.1/4.2 cover climate-change considerations where relevant.
  • Use the new Annex A as an internal training reference to align everyone’s understanding.

五、审核员在转版审核会追问的点

  • Can leadership articulate what “quality culture” concretely means here and how it is measured?
  • Is there documented or minuted evidence that the quality policy aligns with strategic direction?
  • Are risks and opportunities managed separately, and are opportunities actually used in improvement decisions?
  • Do employees understand quality culture and ethical behaviour, not just recite the policy?

ISO 14001:2026 Transition Pack

$19

1 · What actually changed (ISO-confirmed)

  • 4.1 / 4.2 组织环境 — Broader set of environmental conditions: climate change, biodiversity, ecosystems, pollution and resource availability — no longer isolated topics, assessed together as one environmental context.
  • 6.1.4（新增） — Provides a clearer, structured framework for risks and opportunities.
  • 6.3（新增） — Formalizes change management, ensuring environmental considerations stay factored into business decisions.
  • 「外包」→「外部提供的」 — Wording shifts to “externally provided processes, products and services”, extending accountability beyond organizational boundaries.
  • 领导作用 — Stronger accountability: top management must take visible ownership, align objectives with strategy and embed environmental considerations in culture.
  • 9 内部审核 — More rigorous: documented audit programme and defined audit objectives are now required.
  • 管理评审 — More structured management review (three sub-clauses, per ISO convention).
  • Annex A — More active: clarifies concepts such as leadership, culture, environmental responsibility and engagement.
  • 生命周期视角 — Lifecycle perspective further clarified (4.3/6.1).

三、差距自查表（逐项勾销）

  • Expand the 4.1 context analysis to include climate, biodiversity, pollution and resource availability, linked to risks/opportunities.
  • Add controlled documents for 6.1.4 and 6.3: a risk-opportunity framework and a change-management process.
  • Replace every “outsourced” reference with “externally provided” and check supplier/outsourced controls cover boundary-spanning accountability.
  • Strengthen top-management evidence (5): visible ownership, objectives aligned with strategy.
  • Document the audit programme and objectives (9); restructure management review to the new three-part form.

  • Are “externally provided” processes actually controlled, with evidence?
  • Does management review produce verifiable outcomes under the new three-part structure?

Three-System Integrated Transition Pack (9001 + 14001 + 45001)

$49

All three share the Annex SL high-level structure — leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation and improvement are homologous. An integrated transition means one management review covering all three, avoiding three separate projects. Below is a unified timeline and integration guidance; ISO 45001:2027 is still at DIS stage, so its nodes are marked “expected”.

Integration points (get all three right at once)

  • Leadership & culture — 9001’s quality culture/ethics and 14001’s environmental responsibility/culture can merge into one “organizational culture” leadership process; 45001’s safety culture follows the same pattern.
  • Risk & opportunity framework — 14001’s structured 6.1.4 framework can extend into a shared risk-opportunity register (quality / environment / safety).
  • Change management — 14001’s 6.3 change management can serve directly as the shared change-control process across all three.
  • Internal audit & management review — Combine the audit programme and run one management review, producing evidence per each standard’s new structure.

Unified milestone timeline (back-planned from the earliest cutoff)

  • Use 14001’s 6.1.4/6.3 as the shared risk and change framework for all three.
  • For 45001, do “expected” pre-study now; complete it via the free update once published.
