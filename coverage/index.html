Standards coverage

We are honest about depth. Below is exactly how much structure is built in per accreditation body. Where we only have the Global ACI general layer, we say so plainly: field-specific alignment for that body is not available yet, and there is no upload channel that would add it automatically.

Global ACI (Global) (ILAC)

Generic layer — complete structure
Base: ISO/IEC 17025:2017
  • Global ACI TECH-1-009 — Global ACI Policy for Uncertainty in Calibration（原 ILAC P14）

The Global ACI general layer (ISO/IEC 17025 core + Global ACI TECH-1-009, formerly ILAC P14) is built in for every lab; selecting a body adds that country’s supplementary requirements.

CNAS (CN-CNAS)

Full structure
Base: ISO/IEC 17025:2017
  • CNAS-CL01:2018 — Accreditation Criteria for the Competence of Testing and Calibration Laboratories
  • CNAS-CL01-G001:2024 — Application Requirements for CNAS-CL01
  • CNAS-GL011 — Guidance on Internal Audits for Laboratories and Inspection Bodies
  • CNAS-GL012 — Guidance on Management Reviews for Laboratories and Inspection Bodies
  • CNAS-GL042 — Guidance on Intermediate Checks of Measuring Equipment
  • CNAS-RL01 — Rules for Laboratory Accreditation
  • CNAS-RL02 — Rules for Proficiency Testing
  • Global ACI TECH-1-009 — Global ACI Policy for Uncertainty in Calibration（原 ILAC P14）

The CNAS layer is structured: CNAS-CL01-G001:2024 clause-by-clause application requirements (staff qualification, authorised signatories, leased facilities and equipment, 6-year record retention, 12-month internal audit and management review, etc.) plus the GL011 / GL012 / GL042 guidance.

Honesty principle. We never claim field-specific alignment we don’t have. The reality diagnosis, document templates and compliance review cite standard numbers only — no copyrighted text is reproduced. Where a body’s detailed rules are not yet structured, the tool says so outright rather than implying coverage it does not have.
