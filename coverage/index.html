Standards coverage

We are honest about depth. Below is exactly how much structure is built in per accreditation body. Where we only have the ILAC general layer, you can upload the official document through the temporary channel (≤ 24h, not stored) to align field-specific rules.

ILAC (Global) (ILAC)

Generic layer — complete structure
Base: ISO/IEC 17025:2017
  • ILAC-P14 — ILAC Policy for Uncertainty

The ILAC general layer (ISO/IEC 17025 core + ILAC-P14) is built in for every lab; selecting a body adds that country’s supplementary requirements.
