ISO 9001:2026 transition · Document Generation notes

The ISO 9001:2026 document workload is not a full rewrite — it is a targeted revision of what materially changed. The new edition keeps the Annex SL high-level structure and most operation requirements (clause 8) read close to the 2015 text. What actually moves are the policy and terminology in the quality manual, the change-management procedure, the internal-audit plan, the management-review input checklist, the two separate risk and opportunity registers, and awareness training material. This page drafts manuals, procedures, work instructions and forms against the 2026 clauses; the output is editable documented information for you to review and issue under your own document control.

Documents that actually need rewriting

The quality manual: the policy must explicitly connect to context (4.1) and strategic direction, and controlled-document wording needs a review because the 2026 edition writes 20 core terms directly into clause 3, with quality-specific terms entering the main text for the first time. The change-management procedure and its form: write the seven factors of 6.3 into the checklist and add record fields for f) and g). The internal-audit procedure and annual plan: add a mandatory objective column for each audit. The management-review input checklist: add a row for changes in the needs and expectations of interested parties, with an owner and supporting material. Split the risk register and the opportunity register, tying every opportunity to an improvement project, owner and due date. Finally, upgrade awareness training from process briefings to case-based modules on quality culture and ethical behaviour.

The seven factors of clause 6.3

Clause 6.3 requires all seven factors to be weighed before a change: a) purpose and potential consequences; b) impact on QMS integrity; c) availability of resources and information; d) allocation or reallocation of responsibilities and authorities; e) communication of the change; f) how effectiveness will be monitored and evaluated; g) how the results will be reviewed. Most legacy change procedures lack exactly f) and g), the post-change evaluation. When generating the procedure, make these seven the fixed rows of the checklist and give f) and g) their own archivable record forms rather than folding them into a single line saying the change will be reviewed.

Review-and-confirm points, not new gaps

Do not rebuild what was carried over. Design outputs under 8.3 (acceptance criteria, essential characteristics), direct delivery by external providers under 8.4 and availability and use of documented information under 8.5 all read close to the 2015 text — check the clause numbers and citations and move on. Clause 7.1.6 organisational knowledge already existed in 2015; the 2026 edition only shifts the wording from maintain and make available to maintain, apply and, as necessary, share. What you need to add is a sharing mechanism (mentoring, case libraries, rotation debriefs, offboarding handovers) and records of those activities, not a brand-new requirement. Making this distinction saves a large amount of pointless rewriting.

Frequently asked questions

Does the quality manual have to be rewritten from scratch?

Usually not. Focus on the link between policy and strategic direction, terminology and the updated clause references; the structure still follows Annex SL clauses 4 to 10. The genuinely new documented information sits in change planning, the two registers, audit objectives and management-review inputs.

What records does clause 6.3 expect?

At least two: an assessment and approval record covering a) to e) before the change, and separate records for f) monitoring and evaluating effectiveness and g) reviewing the results afterwards. Putting all seven on one sign-off sheet still leaves you without post-change evaluation evidence.

How much of the 2015 documentation can be carried over?

Most clause 8 operational documents can be carried over with a pass over clause numbers and wording; clauses 4 to 7 and clause 9 need substantive revision. Note also that Annex B is removed and a new Annex A of roughly 11 pages clarifies concepts clause by clause — a good reference for aligning internal training.

Can generated documents be submitted for certification as they are?

No. The output is an editable draft generated from the 2026 clauses; you must populate it with your real processes, responsibilities and records, then approve and issue it under your document control. This tool provides no certification, audit or consulting service and issues no certificates.
