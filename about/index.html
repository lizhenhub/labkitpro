About LabKitPro

LabKitPro is a preparation toolkit for testing and calibration laboratories working toward ISO/IEC 17025:2017 accreditation. It turns the repetitive, document-heavy parts of readiness — reality diagnosis, document drafting, compliance screening and uncertainty budgeting — into minutes instead of weeks.

The product is built around one idea: a shared context. You set your country / accreditation body, lab type, stage, party and regulatory scheme once, and every tool aligns its clauses, citations and depth to that context. Nothing about your selection is hidden in a black box — outputs show exactly which standard numbers they reference.

We respect intellectual property strictly: we cite standard numbers and never reproduce copyrighted text, and any document you upload is ephemeral (≤ 24h) and never used for training. LabKitPro produces technical drafts for your preparation; accreditation decisions remain with the relevant body.
