Break a job into steps, list hazards, score severity × likelihood, and rank risks on a 5×5 matrix to prioritise control measures.

Recommendations
  1. The 3 medium-risk item(s) need a permit, a watchman and task-specific training before the job starts.
  2. Re-score with the same matrix after the controls are in place — close the item only when the residual risk drops to acceptable.
  3. Re-run the assessment whenever the job steps, equipment, crew or process change.
