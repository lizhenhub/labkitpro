Conclusions & advice
  1. 1 row(s) with RPN ≥ 100 — act on them first; ties broken by highest Severity.
  2. Any failure mode with S ≥ 9 must have effective prevention or detection regardless of RPN.
  3. Update S/O/D after actions with evidence; the FMEA is a living document — revisit on design/process changes and complaints.
