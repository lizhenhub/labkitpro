Measurement uncertainty: a practical guide

  • Measurement uncertainty = the confidence band around your result; without it, results are not acceptable to any accreditation body.
  • The GUM method (analytical) fits simple, well-modelled quantities; Monte Carlo (MCM) fits non-linear cases with overlapping distributions.
  • Every report/certificate must state uncertainty — a hard §7.6 requirement and a frequent nonconformity at assessment.

The eight accreditation bodies, decoded

What each body adds beyond the common ISO/IEC 17025 clauses, and which documents it mandates — decoded body by body. One path per body: general obligations → core criteria → sector requirements → metrology & PT → application & assessment → maintenance. Find yours and walk its path.

✓This section is free and open — no signup required
1

ILAC

International Laboratory Accreditation CooperationInternational mutual-recognition layer

ILAC is not an accreditation body — it is the global framework for mutual recognition of laboratory accreditation (ILAC MRA). Labs do not apply to ILAC; they are accredited by their national body, which holds ILAC MRA signatory status. On 2026-01-01 ILAC ceased operating and its functions moved to Global ACI (global-aci.org); document numbering is transitioning. The policies below bind labs through their national body.

1General obligations

  • P8 — mandatory before claiming ILAC MRA on export reportsKept effective by Global ACI 2025-29 decision; mapping to MRA-006
  • R7 — pairs with P8; governs the combined markPairs with P8; governs the combined mark
  • P9 — the umbrella framework for PT participationThe umbrella framework for PT participation; → TECH-1-008

2Core criteria

  • No standard of its own — base standards such as ISO/IEC 17025:2017 (testing/calibration) and ISO 15189:2022 (medical) are converted and enforced by each national ABILAC's A-series peer evaluation keeps AB enforcement consistent

3Sector requirements

  • P15 — → TECH-2-001→ TECH-2-001
  • G19G26G7/G29 — only if your field appliesOnly if your field applies
  • G27/G28 — measurement in inspection, scope wording for inspection bodies

4Metrology · uncertainty · PT

  • P10 — → TECH-1-011 in draft; remains effective meanwhile→ TECH-1-011 in draft; remains effective meanwhile
  • P14G17 — calibration vs testing labs respectivelyCalibration vs testing labs respectively
  • G8G18G24 — reporting judgments, scope filing, cal-cycle managementReporting judgments, scope filing, cal-cycle management

5Application & assessment

  • ; MRA eligibility runs through your national body’s IAF/ILAC A2 peer evaluationA2 decides your AB’s signatory status; 2025-09 revision maps to MRA-005

6Maintenance & recent changes

  • ILAC→Global ACI transition: P9→TECH-1-008, P14→TECH-1-009, P10→TECH-1-011 (draft)Watch the new numbers adopted by your AB
  • Archive at ilac.org for reference; live documents at global-aci.org
Key point: At the ILAC layer only three things matter for a lab — traceability (P10), proficiency testing (P9), and symbol & claims (P8/R7). Confirm the current numbering your AB forwards before putting the MRA mark on export reports.

CNAS

China National Accreditation Service for Conformity AssessmentMainland China

The only accreditation body for mainland China, with the most complete file system: general rules (R/RL) + basic criteria (CL) + sector application notes (A) + guides (GL) + explanatory notes (EL) + technical reports (TRL). Step 1 is locking your sector; step 2 is picking the matching A-series.

  • R01R02R03:2026 — R03 revised 2026-09-22, replacing the 2019 editionR03 revised 2026-09-22, replacing the 2019 edition
  • RL03:2025RL04 — fees and acceptance of overseas labs

  • CL01-G001:2024G002G003G004 — G001: 2nd revision with substantive changes in 2024G001: 2nd revision with substantive changes in 2024

3Sector requirements

  • Recent revisions: A004 medical devices (eff. 2026-05-01), A018 construction (eff. 2027-01-01), A016 sensory (2025-01)
  • Special programs S01–S06 (Energy Star, Bluetooth, anti-doping, EPA, OIML…) by business

4Metrology · uncertainty · PT

  • GL006GL025:2023GL042GL054:2023 — calibration labs focusCalibration labs focus

5Application & assessment

  • GL001GL008GL011/GL012:2025 — accreditation guide, NC grading, internal-audit & management-review guides (2025-12)

6Maintenance & recent changes

  • Surveillance per RL01; PT per RL02
  • 2026 additions/revisions: R03:2026, biobank RL10:2026 + EL-30:2026, coal TRL-026/027, advanced manufacturing TRL-024/025, lab animals CL06-A001:2026Technical-report list updated 2026-08-28
Key point: Lock your CL01-A series (40 groups) first, then complete the G-series quartet (G001–G004). Schedule PT early per RL02 — it is the most common pre-assessment failure.

A2LA

American Association for Laboratory AccreditationUSA (non-governmental)

US non-profit, non-governmental accreditation body (ISO/IEC 17011; ILAC/APAC recognition). Files fall into six families: A organization / I information / P policy / R requirements (mandatory) / G guides / F & A308 forms. The R-series are hard requirements; the P-series are policies.

3Sector requirements

  • R208R212R221R255R262R310R311 — pick the R2xx matching your field
  • P103a–e — pick the annex matching your testing fieldPick the annex matching your testing field

6Maintenance & recent changes

Key point: A2LA revises documents densely in 2026. Base your file set on R101 + your sector R2xx + P102/P103; file scope with the F-series lists; F117 is the official carrier of personnel qualification.

UKAS

United Kingdom Accreditation ServiceUnited Kingdom

The UK national accreditation body (ISO/IEC 17011). UKAS labs must comply with three layers simultaneously: UKAS’s own documents (GEN/LAB/TPS) + mandatory EA policies + Global ACI (formerly ILAC) policies. This stacked requirement is UKAS’s signature.

2Core criteria

  • ISO/IEC 17025:2017 / ISO 15189:2022; ISO 15195:2018 for reference-measurement labs
  • LAB 5LAB 12LAB 13LAB 45LAB 48M3003 — reporting and uncertainty series

3Sector requirements

  • LAB 30LAB 33LAB 37LAB 32LAB 201 — sector LAB documents

5Application & assessment

  • Mandatory EA: EA-4/02 calibration MU · EA-3/01 marks & MLA reference · EA-4/17 medical scope · EA-2/15 flexible scope

  • TPS 71LAB 24TPS 47 — medical and under-review documents
Key point: UKAS is about stacking — besides UKAS’s own documents, EA and Global ACI policies are mandatory. Track three publication lists (UKAS M4 publications + EA library + Global ACI library).

NVLAP

National Voluntary Laboratory Accreditation Program · NISTUSA (government program)

A US government program run by NIST under 15 CFR Part 285; criteria aligned to ISO/IEC 17025:2017. Unlike A2LA, NVLAP is a statutorily authorized government program organized by LAP (Laboratory Accreditation Program). Current requirements = project handbook + inserted Lab Bulletins.

1General obligations

  • NIST HB 150 — 2020 edition, Update 1; applies to all programs(2020 edition, Update 1 — applies to all programs)
  • Must insert two current bulletins: LB-156-2024 (program checklists now via NIWS portal) · LB-132-2020 (NIWS root-cause analysis for NCs)

2Core criteria

  • ISO/IEC 17025:2017 aligned; transition bulletins LB-116/117-2020 must be inserted
  • PG-17-2024 — matters for renewal managementMatters for renewal management

3Sector requirements

  • HB 150-x : 150-2 calibration (2024), 150-3 bulk asbestos (2025), 150-5 construction materials, 150-8 acoustics, 150-11 EMC/telecom (FCC/ISED/ASCA), 150-17 crypto security, 150-18 fastener metal (2024), 150-31 health ITPick by LAP
  • Handbook + its Lab Bulletins = current requirements for that program

4Metrology · uncertainty · PT

  • PT per handbook & bulletins: LB-166-2026 asbestos renewal must submit PT recordsKey change for renewal applications
  • Other LAPs set their own PT frequency and records (see handbook)

5Application & assessment

  • On-site checklists (26) distributed via the NIWS portal since 2024 — not public PDFsGeneral criteria checklist (rev. 2026-09-10) available by request from NVLAP
  • Checklists with * work together with a Test Method Review Summary

6Maintenance & recent changes

  • LB-163-2026LB-164-2026LB-165-2026 — recent bulletins
  • Numbering gaps (150-7/9/12…) are terminated programs, no longer listed
Key point: NVLAP’s classic trap is reading the handbook without the bulletins. Handbook + bulletins = current requirements; checklists come only through the NIWS portal — request your authorized account early.

NATA

National Association of Testing Authorities, AustraliaAustralia

Australia’s government-endorsed national body with the broadest coverage: testing / calibration / inspection / medical / GLP / PTP / RMP / biobanking / imaging / sleep / respiratory. Its signature is the Standards Application Document (SAD) — Australia-specific requirements added on top of the international standards.

4Metrology · uncertainty · PT

Key point: Read the SAD first — the Australia-specific layer sits on top of the international standard. Submit scope using the SoA schedule for your industry.
