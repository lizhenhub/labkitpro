Measurement uncertainty: a practical guide

  • Measurement uncertainty = the confidence band around your result; without it, results are not acceptable to any accreditation body.
  • The GUM method (analytical) fits simple, well-modelled quantities; Monte Carlo (MCM) fits non-linear cases with overlapping distributions.
  • Every report/certificate must state uncertainty — a hard §7.6 requirement and a frequent nonconformity at assessment.

ILAC (Global)

ILAC common layer
base: ISO/IEC 17025:2017
Key documents:
  • ILAC-P14 ILAC Policy for Uncertainty — Uncertainty policy (common layer)

CNAS

full local rules
base: ISO/IEC 17025:2017
Key documents:
  • CNAS-CL01:2018 Accreditation Criteria for the Competence of Testing and Calibration Laboratories — Identical adoption of ISO/IEC 17025:2017; basis of CNAS accreditation
  • CNAS-CL01-G001:2024 Application Requirements for CNAS-CL01 — Clause-by-clause China-specific hard requirements (staff qualifications, signatories, 2-year leases, 6-year records, 12-month audit/review cycles)
  • CNAS-GL011 Guidance on Internal Audits for Laboratories and Inspection Bodies — Internal-audit implementation guide
  • CNAS-GL012 Guidance on Management Reviews for Laboratories and Inspection Bodies — Management-review input/output guidance
  • CNAS-GL042 Guidance on Intermediate Checks of Measuring Equipment — Intermediate-check methods for measuring equipment

A2LA

full local rules
base: ISO/IEC 17025:2017
Key documents:
  • A2LA R205 General Requirements for Accreditation — General accreditation requirements
  • A2LA R101 General Requirements: Accreditation of CABs — Institution-level requirements across application, assessment, nonconformity, suspension and withdrawal
  • A2LA R102 Conditions for Accreditation — Conditions: record retention, 30-day change notice, subcontractor notification
  • A2LA R103 General Requirements: Proficiency Testing — Proficiency-testing policy PT1–PT9
  • A2LA R105 Reference to A2LA Accredited Status — Accredited-status claims and symbol-use rules

NVLAP (NIST)

full local rules
base: ISO/IEC 17025:2017
Key documents:
  • NIST HB 150-2020upd1 NVLAP Procedures and General Requirements — Main handbook: full accreditation cycle, PT, traceability Annex B, symbol-use appendix
  • NIST HB 150-2-2024 NVLAP Calibration Laboratories — Calibration specifics: CMC, En criterion, PT frequency
  • NIST HB 150-1-2021 NVLAP Energy Efficient Lighting — Lighting specifics: work instructions, reference-lamp checks, 3-year records
  • NIST HB 150-3-2025 Asbestos Fiber Analysis — Asbestos PT is strictest: all staff participate, no subcontracting
  • NIST PG 17-2024 Expiration as Voluntary Termination — Expiration without renewal counts as voluntary termination

UKAS

full local rules
base: ISO/IEC 17025:2017
Key documents:
  • UKAS GEN 1 General Requirements for Accreditation — General accreditation requirements (incl. PT)
  • UKAS GEN 4 Flexible Scope — Flexible accreditation scope
  • UKAS GEN 6 Use of Accreditation Symbols & Marks — Conditions for using accreditation symbols and marks
  • OPSS National Accreditation Mark Conditions — Statutory conditions for the national accreditation mark
  • UKAS LAB 5 Calibration — Calibration policy (U/M3003)

NATA

full local rules
base: ISO/IEC 17025:2017
Key documents:

  • NATA GAC Traceability Metrological Traceability Policy — Dual traceability paths, three CRM sources, evidence pack
  • NATA GAC PT Proficiency Testing Policy — Documented PT plan and ISO/IEC 17043 provider requirements
  • NATA GAC Equipment Assurance Equipment Assurance & In-house Calibration — Equipment assurance and return-to-service control after repair
  • NATA GAG Balances User Checks of Balances — Balance user checks, performance limits, minimum weight

SINGLAS

full local rules
